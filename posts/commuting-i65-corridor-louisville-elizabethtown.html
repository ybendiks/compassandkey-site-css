Interstate 65 is the main road between Louisville and Elizabethtown, and the towns along it are where many Louisville and Fort Knox commuters choose to live. Locals rarely call it "the I-65 corridor" — they talk about the town: Shepherdsville, Mount Washington, Elizabethtown, Radcliff. This guide walks through each commuter town between Louisville and Elizabethtown, which exits serve it, and what to think about before you buy.

How the drive works

I-65 began as the Kentucky Turnpike, a toll road that opened in 1954 between Louisville and Elizabethtown, and it was the first of Kentucky's toll roads to become free. Today it runs south from Louisville through Bullitt County into Hardin County. Heading south from the Gene Snyder Freeway (I-265), the main exits for commuter towns are:

  • Exit 117 (KY 44): Shepherdsville to the west, Mount Washington to the east
  • Exits 116 and 114: south Shepherdsville via KY 61
  • Exit 112 (KY 245): Clermont and the road to Bardstown
  • Exit 105 (KY 61): Lebanon Junction and Boston
  • Exit 102 (KY 313): Radcliff and Vine Grove, toward Fort Knox
  • Exits 94, 93 and 91: Elizabethtown, plus the Bluegrass Parkway toward Bardstown and Lexington and the Western Kentucky Parkway

US-31W (Dixie Highway) runs roughly parallel to the west, from Louisville past Fort Knox through Radcliff to Elizabethtown. On the east side, US-31E (Bardstown Road) connects Mount Washington with Louisville's southeast side.

Commuter towns between Louisville and Elizabethtown

South Louisville

If you want to stay in Jefferson County, south Louisville neighborhoods near I-65 and the Gene Snyder give you the shortest drive to downtown jobs and the airport, with Louisville's schools, hospitals and amenities close by. See our Louisville page.

Mount Washington

Mount Washington is a Bullitt County city on US-31E, about 20 miles from downtown Louisville. Commuters heading into Louisville's east and southeast side take 31E north across the Gene Snyder, while those heading south toward Elizabethtown or Fort Knox take KY 44 west to I-65 at Exit 117. There has been a lot of new home construction in Mount Washington, and students attend Bullitt County Public Schools, including Bullitt East High School. See our Mount Washington page.

Shepherdsville

Shepherdsville is the Bullitt County seat, sitting right on I-65 about halfway between Louisville and Elizabethtown. Large distribution centers along I-65 in Shepherdsville mean plenty of people work in town, not just in Louisville. KY 44 west from Exit 117 is the route toward US-31W and Fort Knox. See our Shepherdsville page and living in Shepherdsville and Bullitt County.

Hillview, Lebanon Junction and smaller Bullitt County towns

Hillview sits at the north end of Bullitt County, and Lebanon Junction is a small town farther south off Exit 105. Read more in Hillview, KY and Lebanon Junction and West Point.

Radcliff and Vine Grove

For Fort Knox commuters, Radcliff borders the post and Vine Grove sits just west of it. Both are reached from I-65 by KY 313 at Exit 102 or from Louisville by US-31W. Our Fort Knox housing guide covers gates and off-post living in detail.

Elizabethtown

Elizabethtown is the Hardin County seat and the southern anchor of the drive, where I-65 meets the Bluegrass and Western Kentucky parkways. See our Elizabethtown page and moving from Louisville to Elizabethtown.

What to think about before you buy along I-65

  • Drive it at rush hour. Before you write an offer, drive from the house to work on a weekday morning.
  • Check the county line. Bullitt, Hardin and Jefferson counties have different school systems, PVA offices and property taxes. City limits matter too, since some cities levy their own property tax.
  • Check school assignment by address. District lines and school zones don't follow town names. We confirm the assigned schools for every home before you buy.
  • Think about both jobs. If one person works in Louisville and another at Fort Knox, look at towns between the two, like Shepherdsville, Radcliff or Vine Grove.
  • Ask about USDA eligibility. USDA home loan eligibility is set by address, so check each home.
