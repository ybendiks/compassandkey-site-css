Investing in Elizabethtown, KY Real Estate

Elizabethtown is the Hardin County seat, sitting where I-65 meets the Western Kentucky and Bluegrass Parkways, with Fort Knox about 15 miles away and Louisville a straight drive north. That mix of a county seat, an interstate crossroads and a major Army post is what draws most investors here. This page covers what to look at before you buy a rental or investment property in Elizabethtown, and how our team helps.

Fort Knox and rental housing

Soldiers arriving at Fort Knox in-process through the post's Housing Services Office, which keeps listings of off-post rentals and checks that leases include the military provisions soldiers need. Landlords who want military tenants should expect a lease with a military clause. Service members' Basic Allowance for Housing (BAH) is set from local rental costs, and you can look up the current rate for the area on the Defense Travel Management Office's BAH calculator.

What to check before you buy

  • City limits. Properties inside Elizabethtown city limits pay city property tax on top of county taxes. We confirm which side of the line every property is on.
  • Property taxes. The Hardin County PVA assesses each property's value as of January 1, and most bills are collected by the Hardin County Sheriff. Our Elizabethtown property taxes guide explains how it works.
  • School district. Elizabethtown has two public districts, Elizabethtown Independent Schools and Hardin County Schools. District matters to family renters, so check it by address.
  • Commute. For tenants who work at Fort Knox or in Louisville, quick access to I-65, US-31W or the parkways is a real selling point. See our I-65 commuter guide.
  • Condition and inspections. Budget for an inspection on every property, and look closely at roof, HVAC and plumbing on older homes, since repairs come out of your returns.
