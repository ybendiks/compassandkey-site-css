Boston Real Estate FAQ

Is Boston, KY an incorporated city?

No. The Census Bureau counts Boston as a census-designated place, and it isn't on Kentucky's list of incorporated cities, so it has no city government and no city property tax. Nelson County provides local government. Homeowners in Boston pay the state, county, school and district property taxes on the Nelson County bill, which the Nelson County Sheriff's Office collects. The Nelson County PVA assesses property values and takes homestead exemption applications from owners 65 and older or classified as totally disabled.

What schools serve Boston, KY?

Boston is part of Nelson County Schools. Boston School on Wilson Creek Road serves the early grades through 8th grade. Nelson County assigns schools by address, so the assignment depends on the exact home. We confirm the school assignment with the district for every home before you write an offer.

How far is Boston, KY from Elizabethtown and Bardstown?

Boston sits on US-62, which runs west to Elizabethtown and I-65 and east about 12 miles to Bardstown. KY 61 (Lebanon Junction Road) heads north from US-62 at Boston toward I-65 and Shepherdsville. The Bluegrass Parkway is reached from US-62 near Boston at KY 52 or KY 583. Our office is in Elizabethtown, just down US-62, so Boston is close to home for us.

What is Boston, KY known for?

Bourbon and old history. The Booker Noe Distillery is in Boston. Mount Moriah Baptist Church, organized in 1802, is one of the community's oldest institutions. The original settlement, Old Boston, sat a little to the east and probably moved in the mid-1800s to be nearer the new railroad and away from flooding. The Boston post office has been open since 1831.
