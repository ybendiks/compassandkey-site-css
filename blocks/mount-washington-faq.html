Mount Washington Real Estate FAQ

Is Mount Washington, KY a good place to live?

For many buyers, yes. Mount Washington is a Bullitt County city with Louisville directly to the north, so you get a smaller-city setting with a manageable drive to Louisville jobs on US-31E. It's served by Bullitt County Public Schools, with several elementary and middle schools in town and Bullitt East High School on KY 44. The city runs its own parks and recreation department, and Bardstown and Shepherdsville are both close by. Whether it fits you depends on your commute, school zone and the kind of home you want, and we're glad to talk that through.

Is Mount Washington part of Louisville?

No. Mount Washington is its own home rule-class city in Bullitt County, with its own mayor and council. Louisville sits directly to the north in Jefferson County, and downtown Louisville is about 20 miles northwest. Many residents commute into Louisville, which is why Mount Washington is often thought of as a Louisville suburb. For buyers, the practical difference is that homes here fall under Bullitt County government, Bullitt County Public Schools and, inside city limits, the City of Mount Washington.

What is Mount Washington, KY known for?

Its crossroads history. The town began as "The Crossroads," where the old Bardstown-to-Louisville road met the Shepherdsville-to-Shelbyville road, today's US-31E and KY 44. It was incorporated in 1833 and named Mount Washington after the post office rejected "Mount Vernon." In 1862, Confederate cavalry briefly held the town. Three local homes are on the National Register of Historic Places, and the Mount Washington Historical Society cares for the Lloyd House. Today it's a Bullitt County commuter town just south of Louisville.

What schools serve Mount Washington, KY?

Mount Washington is part of Bullitt County Public Schools. Inside the city there are four elementary schools (Old Mill, Mount Washington, Pleasant Grove and Crossroads), two middle schools (Mount Washington Middle and Eastside Middle) and one high school, Bullitt East High School on KY 44 East. Schools are assigned by attendance zone, so homes a few streets apart can feed different elementary or middle schools. We verify the school assignment for each home with the district before you write an offer.

How do property taxes work in Mount Washington?

Homes inside Mount Washington city limits get two bills. The City of Mount Washington sends its own city property tax bill each fall, separate from the county bill, with a discount for paying early. You can pay it at City Hall, by mail or by phone. Separately, the Bullitt County Sheriff's Office collects the state, county, school and district taxes. The Bullitt County PVA assesses property as of January 1, and owners 65 and older or totally disabled apply for the homestead exemption through the PVA.

How far is Mount Washington from Louisville and Elizabethtown?

Downtown Louisville is about 20 miles northwest. Most Louisville commuters take US-31E north, which crosses the Gene Snyder Freeway (I-265) in Jefferson County. For Elizabethtown, KY 44 runs west to Shepherdsville and I-65 at Exit 117, then it's south on the interstate. Bardstown is about 19 miles south on US-31E. When you compare homes, we look at which route you'll drive every day, since being on the right side of town can make a real difference.

What help is available for first-time homebuyers in Mount Washington?

Mount Washington buyers can use Kentucky Housing Corporation (KHC) programs, which include affordable mortgages, down payment assistance, homebuyer education and access to housing counselors. KHC programs are offered through approved lenders, and KHC's website lets you find one and check eligibility. Some buyers also look at USDA loans, but USDA eligibility is set by the property's address on USDA's eligibility map, so it has to be checked home by home. We can connect you with lenders who use these programs and help you shop for homes that fit the loan.
