Living in Bardstown, KY

Bardstown is the county seat of Nelson County and one of the oldest cities in Kentucky, first settled in 1780. It's a small, historic town with a deep bourbon heritage and easy road connections to Louisville, Lexington and Elizabethtown. US-31E, US-62 and US-150 all meet in town, and the Bluegrass Parkway runs just south of the city.

Getting around

  • Louisville: about 45 minutes, using I-65 to the Clermont–Bardstown exit and KY 245, or north on US-31E through Mount Washington
  • Lexington: about an hour east on the Bluegrass Parkway
  • Elizabethtown: west on the Bluegrass Parkway to I-65
  • Nashville: about two hours south

Schools

Two public school districts serve the Bardstown area, and the street address decides which one a home is zoned for. Bardstown City Schools serves most of the city and some of the developed area around it, with Bardstown Early Childhood, Bardstown Primary, Bardstown Elementary, Bardstown Middle and Bardstown High School. Nelson County Schools serves the rest of the county and some Bardstown addresses; its high schools are Nelson County High School and Thomas Nelson High School. Catholic options include St. Joseph School and Bethlehem High School, both in Bardstown.

Choosing where to buy in Bardstown

Locals don't usually shop by subdivision name. They narrow things down with a few practical questions, and we help you answer each one for every home you look at:

  • Inside or outside city limits? Homes inside the city get a City of Bardstown tax bill and city services. Homes outside the city get county services and often more land.
  • Which school district? City limits and school lines don't match exactly. Some homes inside the city are in Nelson County Schools, and some homes outside the city are in Bardstown City Schools.
  • Older or newer? The Bardstown Historic District around downtown is listed on the National Register of Historic Places, with many of the town's oldest homes. If you plan exterior changes there, ask about the city's historic preservation rules first.
  • Which way do you drive? Louisville commuters often look north toward US-31E or KY 245 and I-65. If you'll drive toward Elizabethtown or Lexington, being close to the Bluegrass Parkway exits at US-31E and US-150 saves time.

Work, healthcare and everyday life

Bourbon shapes daily life here. Heaven Hill, Barton 1792, Willett and Lux Row all operate distilleries in and around Bardstown, and the Kentucky Bourbon Festival is the state's official bourbon festival. CommonSpirit Saint Joseph Hospital – Bardstown, offers a 24-hour emergency room, a birth center, cancer care and surgery. For weekends, there's My Old Kentucky Home State Park with the Federal Hill mansion and The Stephen Foster Story, the Basilica of Saint Joseph Proto-Cathedral, the Old Talbott Tavern, and museums including the Oscar Getz Museum of Whiskey and a Civil War museum.
