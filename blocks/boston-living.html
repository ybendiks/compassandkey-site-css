Living in Boston, KY

Boston is a small community in Nelson County on Lick Creek, about 12 miles west of Bardstown. It's a census-designated place, not an incorporated city, and it isn't on Kentucky's list of incorporated cities, so there's no city government and no city property tax. Nelson County provides local government. The first settlement, Old Boston, sat a little to the east. The community probably moved in the mid-1800s to get closer to the new railroad and away from flooding, and the post office has been open since 1831.

Getting around

  • Elizabethtown: west on US-62, which connects to I-65 in Elizabethtown
  • Bardstown: about 12 miles east on US-62
  • Shepherdsville: KY 61 (Lebanon Junction Road) heads north from US-62 at Boston toward I-65 and Shepherdsville
  • Bluegrass Parkway: reached from US-62 near Boston at KY 52 or KY 583

Schools

Boston is served by Nelson County Schools. Boston School on Wilson Creek Road serves the early grades through 8th grade. School assignment depends on the home's address, so we confirm it with the district for each home.
