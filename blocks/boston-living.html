Living in Boston, KY

Boston is a small, unincorporated community in Nelson County on Lick Creek, about 12 miles west of Bardstown. It isn't an incorporated city, so there's no city government and no city property tax. Nelson County provides local government. The first settlement, Old Boston, sat a little to the east. The community probably moved in the mid-1800s to get closer to the new railroad and away from flooding, and the post office has been open since 1831. Boston is quiet, rural country with a working bourbon distillery in its backyard.

Getting around

  • Elizabethtown: west on US-62, which connects to I-65 in Elizabethtown
  • Bardstown: about 12 miles east on US-62
  • Shepherdsville and Louisville: KY 61 (Lebanon Junction Road) heads north from US-62 at Boston toward I-65 and Shepherdsville
  • Bluegrass Parkway: reached from US-62 near Boston at KY 52 or KY 583

Schools

Boston is served by Nelson County Schools. Boston School on Wilson Creek Road serves the early grades through 8th grade and feeds Thomas Nelson High School, which has a Bardstown mailing address. Rural addresses near county lines can be assigned differently, so we confirm the school assignment for each home.

Buying in Boston

Homes here are mostly rural properties, so we check the things that matter out in the county: whether a home is on a septic system, how it gets water, flood zone status near Lick Creek or the Rolling Fork, and whether the address qualifies for a USDA loan. Local landmarks include Beam Suntory's Booker Noe Distillery and Mount Moriah Baptist Church, organized in 1802. For groceries, healthcare and most jobs, residents drive to Bardstown or Elizabethtown.
