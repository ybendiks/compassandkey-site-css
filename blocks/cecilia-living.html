Living in Cecilia, KY

Cecilia is a small, unincorporated community in Hardin County, about 6 miles west of Elizabethtown. It grew up as a railroad stop and was once called Cecilian Junction, after the point where the Illinois Central's Louisville–Paducah main line met a branch line to Hodgenville. Today that main line is run by the Paducah and Louisville Railway. Cecilia has no city government, so buyers here get a rural, west-county setting while staying close to Elizabethtown's jobs and shopping.

Getting around

  • Elizabethtown: about 6 miles east
  • Local roads: KY-86 runs through Cecilia and ends just southeast of the community at US-62. Central Hardin High School and West Hardin Middle School both have Leitchfield Road addresses in Cecilia
  • Fort Knox and Louisville: most drivers go through Elizabethtown to reach I-65 and the routes north

Schools

Cecilia is served by Hardin County Schools. Cecilia Valley Elementary is in the community and serves preschool through 5th grade. Its students go on to West Hardin Middle School, on Leitchfield Road in Cecilia, and then to Central Hardin High School. Central Hardin has a Cecilia mailing address, but the school itself sits inside Elizabethtown city limits. Lakewood Elementary also feeds West Hardin Middle and Central Hardin High. Hardin County assigns schools by each elementary school's street list, so we check every address before you write an offer.

Choosing where to buy in Cecilia

  • Mailing address vs. city limits: A Cecilia mailing address doesn't always mean a home is in the county. Central Hardin High is one example of a Cecilia address inside Elizabethtown city limits. City limits decide whether a home pays city property tax and gets city services, so we confirm them for every home.
  • Which elementary school? Homes around Cecilia can be zoned for Cecilia Valley or a neighboring elementary school, and that choice sets the middle and high school.
  • Land and utilities: Rural homes outside town can come with acreage, wells or septic systems. We check utilities, sewer or septic, and the year built for every home.

Work, healthcare and everyday life

Cecilia is a quiet community, so most residents head to Elizabethtown for shopping, dining and healthcare. Baptist Health Hardin, the regional hospital, is in Elizabethtown. With an elementary school and a middle school in the community and Central Hardin High nearby, families here can keep the school day close to home.
