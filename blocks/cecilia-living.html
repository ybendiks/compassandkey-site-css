Living in Cecilia, KY

Cecilia is a small, unincorporated community in Hardin County, about 6 miles west of Elizabethtown. It grew up as a railroad stop and was once called Cecilian Junction, after the point where the Illinois Central's Louisville–Paducah main line met a branch line to Hodgenville. Today that main line is run by the Paducah and Louisville Railway. Cecilia has no city government, so county government and services apply.

Getting around

  • Elizabethtown: about 6 miles east
  • Local roads: KY-86 runs through Cecilia and ends just southeast of the community at US-62

Schools

Cecilia is served by Hardin County Schools. Cecilia Valley Elementary is in the community and serves preschool through 5th grade. Its students go on to West Hardin Middle School in Cecilia and then to Central Hardin High School. Hardin County assigns schools by each elementary school's street list, so we check every address before you write an offer.
