## Facts used and sources
- Unincorporated community and CDP in Hardin County with no formal city government. About 7 mi NW of Elizabethtown, with Vine Grove to the north, Radcliff to the NE and Cecilia to the south. Represented by a magistrate on the Hardin County Fiscal Court, with law enforcement from KSP Post 4 in Elizabethtown. Served by the volunteer Rineyville Fire Department. Named for Zachariah Riney, Lincoln's first schoolteacher. The Paducah & Louisville Railway runs north to south through it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rineyville,_Kentucky
- KY 220 enters Rineyville, runs concurrent with KY 1600 (Rineyville Road) for about 1 mile, and splits at a roundabout. It ends at US 31W (Dixie Hwy) on the SE side of Radcliff. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentucky_Route_220
- KY 1600 runs from KY 3005 near Elizabethtown to KY 144 at Flaherty. Source: https://wiki.aaroads.com/wiki/Kentucky_Route_1600
- Rineyville Elementary, 275 Rineyville School Road, serves preschool through grade 5. Source: https://rineyville.hardin.kyschools.us/about-us/our-school
- Boundaries are set by elementary street lists. Rineyville Elementary feeds James T. Alton MS → North Hardin HS or Bluegrass MS → John Hardin HS, depending on home location. Source: https://www.hardin.kyschools.us/parents/school-boundaries
- The Chaffee ACP is on US 31 at the Bullion Blvd exit and is open 24/7. Source: https://home.army.mil/knox/about/directions
- PVA assesses most property as of Jan 1, doesn't set rates or collect taxes, and the Hardin County Sheriff's Office collects most bills. Exemptions require a formal application with the PVA. Source: https://www.qpublic.net/ky/hardin/duties.html
- Homestead exemption covers owners 65+ or totally disabled and receiving disability payments. Source: https://revenue.ky.gov/Property/Pages/Property-Tax-Exemptions.aspx
- VA purchase loan: no down payment if the price doesn't exceed the appraised value, no PMI/MIP, seller may pay closing costs, one-time funding fee. Source: https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-types/purchase-loan/
- Carried over from earlier sessions' notes, not re-opened this session: Fort Knox is the region's largest employer (https://hardinchamber.com/fort-knox-is-the-largest-employer/). Ireland Army Health Clinic serves soldiers, families and retirees with TRICARE (https://ireland.tricare.mil/About-Us). Baptist Health Hardin is in Elizabethtown (see etown/vine-grove notes).
