## Facts used and sources
- Unincorporated community in Hardin County, in the Elizabethtown MSA, ZIP 42740. First named Walker's Station for Lewis B. Walker's store. Renamed for its new train station on the L&N Railroad. The town had an elementary and a high school, and high schoolers were consolidated at Central Hardin HS. East Hardin Middle (on the Glendale campus) closed and relocated to Elizabethtown. I-65 Exit 86 is a new interchange. About a mile of KY 222 was widened to four lanes across the interchange, and US 31W (S Dixie Hwy) intersects it. The Glendale Crossing Festival is held the third Saturday of October and is known for antiques and crafts. The Whistle Stop is along the railroad in a former hardware store and has served southern-style food since 1975. The megasite hosts the BlueOval SK Battery Park. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glendale,_Kentucky
- "About five miles south of Elizabethtown". Source: https://kyatlas.com/ky-glendale.html
- Glendale Historic District: NRHP listed in 1988, 21.5 acres along Main St, 34 contributing buildings, late 19th and early 20th century residential and commercial architecture. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glendale_Historic_District_(Glendale,_Kentucky)
- Antique malls within walking distance of the tracks, on Railroad Ave and Main St. Sources: https://hobbiesonabudget.com/?p=678538 ; https://www.sokyhappenings.com/?p=3121 ("more than a dozen antique and gift shops" in about two blocks)
- Hardin Schools boundaries follow elementary street lists. Source: https://www.hardin.kyschools.us/parents/school-boundaries
- Chaffee ACP on US 31 at the Bullion Blvd exit. Source: https://home.army.mil/knox/about/directions
