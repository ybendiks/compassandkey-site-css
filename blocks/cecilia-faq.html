Cecilia Real Estate FAQ

Is Cecilia, KY a good place to live?

That depends on what you're looking for. Cecilia is an unincorporated Hardin County community about 6 miles west of Elizabethtown, so there's no city government or city property tax. Cecilia Valley Elementary and West Hardin Middle School are both in the community, and students go on to Central Hardin High School. It grew up as a railroad stop once called Cecilian Junction. Is Cecilia, KY its own city?

No. Cecilia is an unincorporated community and census-designated place in Hardin County, so it has no city government and no city property tax. County government and services apply. The community grew up as a railroad stop, once called Cecilian Junction, where the Illinois Central's Louisville–Paducah main line met a branch line to Hodgenville. What schools serve Cecilia, KY?

Cecilia is served by Hardin County Schools. Cecilia Valley Elementary serves preschool through 5th grade, and its students go on to West Hardin Middle School in Cecilia and then to Central Hardin High School. The district assigns schools by each elementary school's street list, so the assignment depends on the home's address. We check the exact address with the district before you write an offer.

How do property taxes work for a Cecilia home?

Homes in unincorporated Cecilia don't pay city property tax. Their bills cover county, school and other local taxing districts. The Hardin County Property Valuation Administrator (PVA) assesses most property as of January 1, but it doesn't set tax rates. The Hardin County Sheriff's Office collects most bills. Owners who are 65 or older or totally disabled can apply for Kentucky's homestead exemption through the Hardin County PVA.

What loan options should Cecilia homebuyers look at?

It's worth checking any Cecilia home against the USDA's property eligibility tool. USDA guaranteed loans require the home to be in an eligible rural area and the household to be under an income limit. First-time and repeat buyers can also use Kentucky Housing Corporation programs through participating lenders, and KHC down payment assistance is paired with a KHC first mortgage. Eligible veterans and service members can use VA loans.
