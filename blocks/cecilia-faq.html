Cecilia Real Estate FAQ

Is Cecilia, KY a good place to live?

Cecilia can be a good fit for buyers who want a small, rural community close to town. It's an unincorporated Hardin County community about 6 miles west of Elizabethtown, and an elementary school and a middle school sit right in the community. The tradeoff is that most shopping, dining and medical care are in Elizabethtown, so plan on a short drive for errands. We can walk you through how Cecilia compares with homes closer to town.

Is Cecilia, KY its own city?

No. Cecilia is an unincorporated community and census-designated place in Hardin County, so it has no city government and no city property tax. Some properties with a Cecilia mailing address actually fall inside Elizabethtown city limits. Central Hardin High School is one example: it has a Cecilia address but sits within the city. Before you make an offer, we confirm whether a home is inside or outside city limits.

What schools serve Cecilia, KY?

Cecilia is served by Hardin County Schools. Cecilia Valley Elementary serves preschool through 5th grade, and its students go on to West Hardin Middle School on Leitchfield Road in Cecilia and then to Central Hardin High School. The district assigns schools by each elementary school's street list, so a home near Cecilia may be zoned for a different elementary school. We check the exact address with the district before you write an offer.

How do property taxes work for a Cecilia home?

Homes in unincorporated Cecilia don't pay city property tax. Their bills cover county, school and other local taxing districts. The Hardin County Property Valuation Administrator (PVA) assesses most property as of January 1, but it doesn't set tax rates. The Hardin County Sheriff's Office collects most bills. If a home with a Cecilia address is inside Elizabethtown city limits, it also owes city tax. Owners who are 65 or older or totally disabled can apply for Kentucky's homestead exemption through the Hardin County PVA.

How far is Cecilia from Elizabethtown and Fort Knox?

Cecilia is about 6 miles west of Elizabethtown. KY-86 runs through the community and ends just southeast of it at US-62. From Cecilia, Fort Knox and Louisville are usually reached by driving through Elizabethtown, so a home on the east side of the area can shorten a daily commute. If you're relocating on military orders, we'll help you compare Cecilia with communities closer to the post.

What loan options should Cecilia homebuyers look at?

Cecilia is rural, so it's worth checking any home there against the USDA's property eligibility tool. USDA guaranteed loans require the home to be in an eligible rural area and the household to be under an income limit. First-time and repeat buyers can also use Kentucky Housing Corporation programs through participating lenders, and KHC down payment assistance is paired with a KHC first mortgage. Eligible veterans and service members can use VA loans. We'll connect you with local lenders who offer these programs.
