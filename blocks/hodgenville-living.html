Living in Hodgenville, KY

Hodgenville is the county seat of LaRue County and a small, rural town in the valley of the North Fork of the Nolin River, about 12 miles southeast of Elizabethtown. It's best known as the birthplace of Abraham Lincoln, and life here centers on the courthouse town square and the farmland around it. LaRue County is part of the Elizabethtown–Fort Knox metro area, so many buyers choose Hodgenville for a quieter, small-town setting while staying within reach of Hardin County jobs and shopping.

Getting around

  • Elizabethtown: about 12 miles northwest on KY-61 (Lincoln Parkway), which also connects to I-65 at Exit 91
  • Bardstown: about 24 miles northeast on US-31E
  • Glasgow: about 43 miles south on US-31E
  • Louisville: north by way of KY-61 and I-65; Louisville International Airport is about 55 miles from the Lincoln Birthplace just south of town
  • Fort Knox: reached through Elizabethtown, so commuters usually head out on KY-61

Schools

All of LaRue County is served by one public district, LaRue County Schools, and its schools are all located in Hodgenville: LaRue County High School and LaRue County Middle School on South Lincoln Boulevard, Hodgenville Elementary School, Abraham Lincoln Elementary School on Lincoln Farm Road, and a district preschool on College Street. Because every address in the county feeds the same middle and high school, school choice here mostly comes down to which elementary school a home is zoned for. When that matters to you, we confirm a home's elementary assignment with the district before you write an offer.

Choosing where to buy in Hodgenville

Buyers here don't shop by subdivision name. They narrow things down with a few practical questions, and we help you answer each one for every home you look at:

  • Inside or outside city limits? Homes inside Hodgenville pay city property tax and get city services such as city water, police and fire. Outside town, you'll find more rural homes, farmland and acreage under county services.
  • Which elementary school? The district is countywide, so the elementary attendance zone is the school question to check.
  • Established home or land? The area offers established in-town neighborhoods as well as land for buyers who want a few acres or more. We check the year built, utilities and septic or sewer for each home so you know what you're buying.
  • Which way do you drive? If you work in Elizabethtown, at Fort Knox or in Louisville, being close to KY-61 shortens the drive. If you head toward Bardstown or south toward Glasgow, US-31E is the road to watch.

Work, healthcare and everyday life

Hodgenville is a small town, and many residents drive to Elizabethtown for work, shopping and services. For healthcare, Baptist Health Family Medicine – Hodgenville offers primary care in town, and Baptist Health Hardin, the regional hospital in Elizabethtown, serves LaRue County. Day to day, you have the LaRue County Public Library, the Lincoln Museum on the town square with its bronze Lincoln statues, the Abraham Lincoln Birthplace National Historical Park just south of town, and the park's Boyhood Home Unit at Knob Creek on US-31E. Each October, the town hosts Lincoln Days, a two-day festival with a parade, pioneer games and the Railsplitter Run.
