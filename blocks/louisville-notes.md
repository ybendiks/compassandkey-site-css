## Facts used and sources
- Kentucky's largest city; nicknames Derby City, River City, Gateway to the South, Falls City, The 'Ville; city-county merger January 6, 2003 forming Louisville/Jefferson County Metro Government; other incorporated places excluded from the "balance"; I-64/I-65/I-71 meet in Louisville; Louisville Muhammad Ali International Airport hosts the UPS worldwide hub; major employers UPS, Norton Healthcare, JCPS, UofL Health, Ford (two plants), Baptist Health, GE Appliances (HQ), Humana; Olmsted-designed parks; Waterfront Park on the Ohio River hosts concerts and festivals; Jefferson Memorial Forest has trails; Kentucky Derby first Saturday of May at Churchill Downs, ending the two-week Kentucky Derby Festival; UofL, Bellarmine, Spalding; West/South/East End; Highlands with Bardstown Road at its heart; Old Louisville is the largest Victorian historic district — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisville,_Kentucky
- Cities in Jefferson County other than Louisville kept their status after the merger — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisville_Metro
- South End is south and southwest of downtown; Old Louisville and the Highlands are in the inner area between the East and South Ends; Butchertown, Germantown, Portland are known for shotgun houses; newer areas on former farms have curving streets and cul-de-sacs — https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Cityscape_of_Louisville,_Kentucky
- Iroquois Park is on the south side; Olmsted designed Iroquois, Cherokee and Shawnee parks — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iroquois_Park
- Jefferson Memorial Forest is in southwest Louisville — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jefferson_Memorial_Forest
- I-264 is a partial loop; Shawnee Expressway (western part) and Watterson Expressway — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_264_(Kentucky)
- I-265 is the Gene Snyder Freeway, the outer beltway — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_265
- US-31W is known as Dixie Highway; runs south from Louisville past Fort Knox, through Radcliff, to Elizabethtown — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_31W ; Radcliff is about 34 mi south of Louisville on 31W, and Fort Knox borders it — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radcliff,_Kentucky
- Elizabethtown is about 45 minutes south on I-65 — from the existing etown-living.html block (Wikipedia Elizabethtown page says I-65 runs 30 mi NNE to Louisville)
- Jefferson County Sheriff's Office is the main property tax collector (state, metro, school, fire, special districts, including the Louisville/Jefferson County Urban-Service District); bills mailed by November 1; early-payment discount; value as of January 1; many smaller home rule cities issue and collect separate tax bills — https://jeffersonsheriffky.gov/property-tax/faq
- Urban Service District = old City of Louisville boundaries; taxing jurisdictions depend on property location — https://jeffersonpva.ky.gov/faq-topics/taxes/page/2/
- Assessment date January 1 (KRS 132.220(1)(a)) — https://www.jeffersonpva.ky.gov/faq-topics/taxes/
- Homestead exemption for owners 65+ on their primary residence; disability exemption for owners who are 100% disabled and live in the home; only one exemption per household; PVA property search — https://jeffersonpva.ky.gov/
- JCPS managed choice by home address; magnet and optional programs; application period in late fall, closes in December; not first come, first served; School Finder — https://jefferson.kyschools.us/o/jcps/page/student-assignment
- Resides school is the default; Choice Zone with no default school; magnets include duPont Manual, Louisville Male, Central High School Magnet Career Academy — https://jefferson.kyschools.us/high-school-choices
- Louisville Metro Down Payment Assistance: income limits, not limited to first-time buyers but buyer may not currently own a home, HUD-approved counseling required, home must be in Louisville Metro, application window opens periodically — https://louisvilleky.gov/government/housing/down-payment-assistance-program
- KHC loans, down payment assistance, homebuyer education, approved lenders — https://www.kyhousing.org/Homeownership/Pages/default.aspx
- Seasonality (fastest sales around May, price peak around June, slowest December to February) — Offerpad analysis of Redfin Data Center data for 15 metros: https://www.offerpad.com/articles/best-time-to-sell-a-house/
- Hardin County has its own school districts and PVA — existing Elizabethtown blocks (etown-living.html / etown-faq.html)
