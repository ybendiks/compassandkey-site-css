Living in Bee Spring, KY

Bee Spring is a small, unincorporated community in northern Edmonson County, along KY-259 about seven miles north of Brownsville, the county seat. There's no city government here, and the post office has been open since 1854. Nolin River Lake and Nolin Dam are just southeast of town, and Nolin Lake State Park sits on the northern edge of Mammoth Cave National Park.

Getting around

  • Leitchfield and Elizabethtown: KY-259 runs north past the Nolin Lake area into Grayson County and Leitchfield, where it meets the Western Kentucky Parkway at Exit 107. From there, take the parkway east to Elizabethtown.
  • Brownsville and Bowling Green: KY-259 south to Brownsville. Bee Spring is part of the Bowling Green metro area.
  • Nolin Lake: KY-728 begins just south of Bee Spring and leads to Nolin Dam and Nolin Lake State Park.
  • Time zone: Edmonson County is on Central time, an hour behind Elizabethtown.

Schools

Edmonson County Schools serves Bee Spring. The district has two elementary schools, Kyrock Elementary in the northern part of the county and South Edmonson Elementary in the south, and its middle and high schools, Edmonson County Middle School and Edmonson County High School, are in Brownsville. School assignment depends on the home's address, so we confirm it with the district for each home before you write an offer.

Choosing where to buy around Bee Spring

  • No city limits to check: Bee Spring is unincorporated, so there's no city property tax and county services apply.
  • Near the lake: Nolin River Lake is a U.S. Army Corps of Engineers lake, and the Corps' Nolin River Lake office is on Nolin Dam Road in Bee Spring.
