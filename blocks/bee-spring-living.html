Living in Bee Spring, KY

Bee Spring is a small, unincorporated community in northern Edmonson County, along KY-259 about seven miles north of Brownsville, the county seat. There's no city government here, just a rural crossroads with a post office that has been open since 1854. What draws buyers is the location: Nolin River Lake and Nolin Dam are just southeast of town, and Nolin Lake State Park sits on the northern edge of Mammoth Cave National Park.

Getting around

  • Leitchfield and Elizabethtown: KY-259 runs north past the Nolin Lake area into Grayson County and Leitchfield, where it meets the Western Kentucky Parkway at Exit 107. From there, take the parkway east to Elizabethtown.
  • Brownsville and Bowling Green: KY-259 south to Brownsville. Bee Spring is part of the Bowling Green metro area.
  • Nolin Lake: KY-728 begins just south of Bee Spring and leads to Nolin Dam and Nolin Lake State Park.
  • Time zone: Edmonson County is on Central time, an hour behind Elizabethtown.

Schools

Edmonson County Schools serves Bee Spring. Kyrock Elementary, the district's northern elementary school, is on KY-259 just south of Bee Spring, and older students attend the Edmonson County 5/6 Center, Edmonson County Middle School and Edmonson County High School in Brownsville. We confirm school assignment with the district for each home before you write an offer.

Choosing where to buy around Bee Spring

  • Lake, park or road frontage? Some buyers want to be close to Nolin Lake or the national park; others want land along the main roads for an easier daily drive.
  • Lake rules: Nolin is a U.S. Army Corps of Engineers lake. Private docks and other uses of Corps shoreline need a permit or license, so we check what's allowed before you buy.
  • Rural systems: Because there's no city here, we check road access, utilities and whether a home uses a septic system as part of every purchase.
