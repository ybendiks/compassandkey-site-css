Living in Radcliff, KY

Radcliff sits in northern Hardin County, bordered by Fort Knox to the north and Vine Grove to the west. The town grew up alongside the post: it was laid out in 1919 and named for Major William Radcliffe of the Quartermaster Corps at Camp Henry Knox, and it was incorporated in 1956. Today it's a military community through and through, with soldiers, civilian employees, retirees and their families as neighbors.

Getting around

  • Fort Knox: right next door. The main Chaffee gate is off US-31W at the Bullion Boulevard exit, just north of town
  • Elizabethtown: about 11 miles south on US-31W (Dixie Highway)
  • Louisville: about 34 miles north on US-31W
  • I-65: about 9 miles east via KY-313, the Joe Prather Highway

Schools

Radcliff is served by Hardin County Schools. Most students in the city attend North Hardin High School, and some addresses are zoned for John Hardin High School. North Middle School and elementary schools including North Park, Woodland, Meadow View and Radcliff Elementary are also in the area. North Hardin Christian School is a private, all-grades option. Families living on Fort Knox are zoned to the post's Department of Defense Education Activity (DoDEA) schools, so if you're deciding between on-post and off-post housing, schools are part of that choice. The Fort Knox School Liaison Officer can help with registration either way.

When school zoning matters to you, we check each home against the district's attendance boundaries before you write an offer.

Choosing where to buy in Radcliff

Most buyers here don't shop by subdivision name. They narrow things down by a few practical questions, and we help you answer each one for every home you look at:

  • Inside or outside city limits? Homes inside Radcliff pay city property tax and get city services. Nearby homes in the county or in Vine Grove follow different rules, so we confirm which applies to each address.
  • Which high school? The address decides whether a home feeds North Hardin or John Hardin.
  • Older or newer? Radcliff grew in waves with Fort Knox, so you'll find established homes from earlier decades as well as newer construction. We look up each home's build year and what's been updated.
  • Which way do you drive? If you work on post, being close to US-31W and the gates can shorten your day. If you commute to Elizabethtown or Louisville, access to 31W or the Joe Prather Highway to I-65 matters more.

Work, healthcare and everyday life

Fort Knox is the region's largest employer, and the Army's Human Resources Command moving to the post helped steady Radcliff's growth. The regional hospital, Baptist Health Hardin, is a short drive south in Elizabethtown, and eligible military families can also use Ireland Army Health Clinic on Fort Knox. The city runs Saunders Springs Nature Preserve, which has wooded trails and a waterfall, plus Radcliff City Park, Dawley Park, and the Colvin Community Center and its summer pool. The Kentucky Veterans Cemetery Central and the Kentucky September 11th Memorial are both on North Dixie Boulevard.
