Rineyville Real Estate FAQ

Is Rineyville, KY a good place to live?

It suits buyers who want a quieter, rural setting with an easy drive to work. Rineyville is an unincorporated Hardin County community about 7 miles northwest of Elizabethtown. KY-220 connects it to Radcliff and US-31W toward Fort Knox. Rineyville Elementary is right in the community, and county services cover the area. The tradeoff is that most shopping, dining and healthcare are in Elizabethtown or Radcliff. We can help you weigh that against your commute.

Is Rineyville, KY a city?

No. Rineyville is an unincorporated community and census-designated place in Hardin County, so it has no city government. A magistrate on the Hardin County Fiscal Court represents the area, Kentucky State Police Post 4 in Elizabethtown handles law enforcement, and the volunteer Rineyville Fire Department provides fire protection. For buyers, this means there's no city property tax, and county rules and services apply. We confirm every home's address details before you make an offer.

What schools serve Rineyville, KY homes?

Rineyville is part of Hardin County Schools. Rineyville Elementary on Rineyville School Road serves preschool through 5th grade. After that, the district splits students by home location: some attend James T. Alton Middle and North Hardin High, and others attend Bluegrass Middle and John Hardin High. The district sets boundaries from each elementary school's street list, so we check the exact address before you write an offer.

How do property taxes work for a Rineyville home?

Rineyville is unincorporated, so there's no city property tax. Your bill covers county, school and other local taxing districts. The Hardin County Property Valuation Administrator (PVA) assesses most property as of January 1, but it doesn't set tax rates. The Hardin County Sheriff's Office collects most tax bills. Kentucky's homestead exemption is available to owners who are 65 or older or totally disabled, and you apply for it with the Hardin County PVA.

How far is Rineyville from Fort Knox?

Rineyville is on the Radcliff side of Hardin County. KY-220 runs east from the community to US-31W (Dixie Highway) on the southeast edge of Radcliff. Fort Knox's main Chaffee gate is on US-31W at the Bullion Boulevard exit, and it's open 24 hours. Elizabethtown is about 7 miles southeast by KY-1600. If you're on PCS orders, we'll help you look at homes along the route that fits your duty location.

Can I use a VA loan to buy a home in Rineyville?

Yes. VA purchase loans work for eligible Rineyville homes just as they do anywhere else. According to VA.gov, you may not need a down payment if the sale price doesn't exceed the appraised value, there's no private mortgage insurance, and the seller may pay some of the closing costs. A one-time VA funding fee may apply. Many buyers on this side of Hardin County have ties to Fort Knox, and we can connect you with local lenders who handle VA loans.
