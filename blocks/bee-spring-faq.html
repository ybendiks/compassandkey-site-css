Bee Spring Real Estate FAQ

Is Bee Spring, Kentucky a good place to live?

That depends on what you're looking for. Bee Spring is a small, unincorporated community in northern Edmonson County, about seven miles north of Brownsville on KY-259, and part of the Bowling Green metro area. Nolin River Lake and Nolin Dam are just southeast of town, and Nolin Lake State Park sits on the northern edge of Mammoth Cave National Park. If the location suits you, we're happy to help you find the right spot.

Is Bee Spring, KY an incorporated city, and how do property taxes work?

No. Bee Spring is unincorporated, so there's no city government and no city property tax. The Edmonson County Property Valuation Administrator (PVA) assesses each property's value, and the Edmonson County Sheriff's Office collects county property tax bills. Kentucky offers a homestead exemption for homeowners 65 and older and for those classified as totally disabled, which you apply for through the PVA.

What schools serve Bee Spring, KY?

Bee Spring is part of Edmonson County Schools. The district has two elementary schools, Kyrock Elementary in the northern part of the county and South Edmonson Elementary in the south. Edmonson County Middle School and Edmonson County High School are in Brownsville. School assignment depends on the home's address, so we confirm it with the district for each home before you make an offer.

How do you get from Bee Spring to Elizabethtown?

KY-259 runs north from Bee Spring through Grayson County to Leitchfield, where it meets the Western Kentucky Parkway at Exit 107; from there the parkway runs east to Elizabethtown. Edmonson County is on Central time and Elizabethtown is on Eastern, so plan meetings and closings with the hour difference in mind. Heading south, KY-259 leads to Brownsville, and Bee Spring is part of the Bowling Green metro area.
