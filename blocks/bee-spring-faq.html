Bee Spring Real Estate FAQ

Is Bee Spring, Kentucky a good place to live?

For buyers who want quiet, rural living near the water, it can be. Bee Spring is a small, unincorporated community in northern Edmonson County, about seven miles north of Brownsville on KY-259, with Nolin River Lake and Nolin Lake State Park close by and Mammoth Cave National Park just beyond. You'll drive to Brownsville, Leitchfield or Bowling Green for most shopping and services. If that tradeoff suits you, we're happy to help you find the right spot.

Is Bee Spring, KY an incorporated city, and how do property taxes work?

No. Bee Spring is unincorporated, so there's no city government and no city property tax. The Edmonson County Property Valuation Administrator (PVA) assesses each property's value, the Edmonson County Sheriff's Office collects county property tax bills, and unpaid bills are later turned over to the County Clerk. Kentucky offers a homestead exemption for homeowners 65 and older and for those classified as totally disabled, which you apply for through the PVA.

What schools serve Bee Spring, KY?

Bee Spring is part of Edmonson County Schools. Kyrock Elementary, the district's elementary school for the northern part of the county, is on KY-259 just south of Bee Spring. Older students attend the Edmonson County 5/6 Center, Edmonson County Middle School and Edmonson County High School in Brownsville. If a particular school matters to you, we'll confirm assignment with the district for each home before you make an offer.

What should I know about buying lake property near Bee Spring?

Nolin River Lake is a U.S. Army Corps of Engineers lake, and the Corps' Nolin River Lake office is on Nolin Dam Road in Bee Spring. The Corps manages the shoreline, and private docks and other uses of Corps land need a permit or license under the lake's Shoreline Management Plan. Owning a home near the water doesn't by itself include a dock. We also help you check road access, utilities and septic before you buy.

How do you get from Bee Spring to Elizabethtown?

Most drivers take KY-259 north through Grayson County to Leitchfield, then the Western Kentucky Parkway east from Exit 107 to Elizabethtown. Edmonson County is on Central time and Elizabethtown is on Eastern, so plan meetings and closings with the hour difference in mind. Heading south, KY-259 leads to Brownsville, and Bee Spring is part of the Bowling Green metro area.
