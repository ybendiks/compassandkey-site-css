Bardstown Real Estate FAQ

Is Bardstown, KY a good place to live?

For many buyers, yes. Bardstown is the county seat of Nelson County, a small, historic town with two public school districts, Catholic schools and a hospital with a 24-hour emergency room. It's within about 45 minutes of Louisville and about an hour of Lexington, and the Bluegrass Parkway connects it to Elizabethtown. It's a small town, not a suburb, so it suits buyers who want that pace. Whether it fits you depends on your commute, schools and budget, and we're happy to talk it through.

What is Bardstown, KY known for?

Bourbon and history. Bardstown holds the trademarked title "Bourbon Capital of the World," is home to the Kentucky Bourbon Festival, and has distilleries including Heaven Hill, Barton 1792, Willett and Lux Row. Settled in 1780, it's one of Kentucky's oldest cities and was the first center of Catholicism west of the Appalachians. Landmarks include My Old Kentucky Home State Park (Federal Hill), the Basilica of Saint Joseph Proto-Cathedral, the Old Talbott Tavern and the Bardstown Historic District.

Which school district serves a Bardstown address?

It depends on the exact address. Bardstown City Schools serves most of the city and some nearby developed areas, and its high school is Bardstown High School. Nelson County Schools serves the rest of the county and some Bardstown addresses, with Nelson County High School and Thomas Nelson High School. City limits and school lines don't match exactly, so a "Bardstown" mailing address doesn't tell you the district. We confirm the district for every home before you make an offer.

How do property taxes work in Bardstown and Nelson County?

The Nelson County Property Valuation Administrator (PVA) assesses every property; the PVA doesn't collect taxes. Bardstown owners can get up to three bills: a county bill from the Nelson County Sheriff, a City of Bardstown bill for homes inside city limits, and a Bardstown City Schools bill, collected by the school board's tax office, for homes in that district. Kentucky's homestead exemption is available to owners 65 and older or classified as totally disabled, and you apply through the PVA. Check the sheriff's and school district's sites for current rates and due dates.

Is it expensive to live in Bardstown?

It depends on the home and where it sits. Bardstown has historic homes near downtown, established neighborhoods across town and homes on more land out in Nelson County, so prices cover a wide range. Location also affects your tax bills, because homes inside city limits and in the Bardstown City Schools district get extra bills. Is Bardstown, Kentucky a good place to retire?

Many retirees like it. Bardstown has a hospital in town, CommonSpirit Saint Joseph Hospital – Bardstown, with a 24-hour emergency room, cancer care and surgery. It also has a historic downtown, museums and My Old Kentucky Home State Park, and Louisville is about 45 minutes away for specialists and the airport. Kentucky homeowners 65 and older can apply to the Nelson County PVA for the homestead exemption. We can help you find a single-level home close to the things you use most.

How long is the commute from Bardstown to Louisville?

Plan on about 45 minutes. Most drivers take KY 245 to I-65 at the Clermont–Bardstown exit, and others go north on US-31E through Mount Washington. Lexington is about an hour east on the Bluegrass Parkway, and the same parkway runs west to Elizabethtown and I-65. If you'll drive to Louisville every day, homes on the side of town nearest US-31E or KY 245 can save time, and we'll map the drive for every home you consider.

What help is available for first-time homebuyers in Bardstown?

Bardstown buyers can use Kentucky Housing Corporation (KHC) programs: home loans for first-time and repeat buyers, down payment assistance paired with a KHC mortgage, and homebuyer education. These are offered through participating lenders that serve Nelson County. If you're looking outside city limits, check the address on the USDA eligibility map, since USDA loan eligibility depends on location. We can connect you with local lenders who use these programs regularly.
