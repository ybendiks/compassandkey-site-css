## Facts used + sources
- In northern Hardin County; Fort Knox borders it to the north and Vine Grove to the west. Laid out in 1919 by Horace McCullum and named for Major William Radcliffe of the Quartermaster Corps at Camp Henry Knox. Incorporated March 1956. Economy tied to Fort Knox; BRAC 2005 and the Human Resources Command move made the population larger and more stable. US-31W runs on the east side. About 34 mi to Louisville and about 11 mi to Elizabethtown via US-31W. Most students attend North Hardin HS and some are zoned to John Hardin HS. North Middle; North Park, Woodland, Meadowview and Radcliff Elementary; North Hardin Christian School (private, all grades). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radcliff,_Kentucky
- North Hardin HS is part of Hardin County Public Schools, at 801 S Logsdon Pkwy, Radcliff. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Hardin_High_School
- John Hardin HS is in Radcliff but uses an Elizabethtown mailing address; it is in Hardin County Schools. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hardin_High_School
- Hardin County Schools school list (spelled "Meadow View"). Fort Knox residents are zoned to DoDEA schools. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardin_County_Schools
- Radcliff Elementary is a Hardin County Schools school and has "Military Information" pages. Source: https://radcliff.hardin.kyschools.us/
- Fort Knox schools are DoDEA. The School Liaison Officer helps with registration for both DoD and local schools. Source: https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/fort-knox/education/education
- Chaffee access control point is on US-31 at the Bullion Blvd exit. Source: https://home.army.mil/knox/about/directions
- Joe Prather Highway is the KY-313 stretch from I-65 (exit 102) to Flaherty Rd, through Radcliff and Vine Grove. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentucky_Route_313
- I-65 to Radcliff is about 9 miles via Joe Prather Hwy, meeting Dixie Hwy (31W). Ireland Army Health Clinic is on Fort Knox. Source: https://ireland.tricare.mil/Getting-Care/Driving-Directions
- Fort Knox is the region's largest employer (guest column by the Knox Regional Development Alliance CEO). Source: https://hardinchamber.com/fort-knox-is-the-largest-employer/
- City parks: Colvin Community Center (opened 1979) and its pool (open Memorial Day to Labor Day), Dawley Park, Duvall Park, Radcliff City Park, and Saunders Springs Nature Preserve (trails, waterfall, no charge). Source: https://radcliff.org/parks-recreation/
- Kentucky Museum of American Pocketknives is listed as a Radcliff attraction. Source: https://radcliff.org/about/visit-radcliff/
- Kentucky Veterans Cemetery Central is in Radcliff (directions point to 2501 N Dixie Blvd). Source: https://veterans.ky.gov/Cemeteries/Pages/Kentucky-Veterans-Cemetery-Central.aspx
- The Kentucky September 11th Memorial is at Kentucky Veterans Cemetery Central, 2501 N Dixie. It includes a WTC beam and limestone from the Pentagon. Source: https://radclifftourism.org/?p=1792
- The City of Radcliff Finance Office is "responsible for billing, collecting..." all city revenues, including property tax and tax roll preparation. Source: https://radcliff.org/finance-office/. Delinquent city tax bills are payable to the City of Radcliff: https://radcliff.org/4152-2
- The Hardin County PVA assesses fair market value as of January 1, does not set rates or collect taxes, and the Sheriff's Office collects most bills. Source: https://qpublic.net/ky/hardin/duties.html
- Homestead exemption covers owners 65 and older or classified as totally disabled, filed with the county PVA. Source: https://revenue.ky.gov/Property/Residential-Farm-Commercial-Property/Pages/Homestead-Exemption.aspx
- VA purchase loan: no down payment if the price doesn't exceed the appraised value, no PMI, seller may pay closing costs, one-time funding fee. Source: https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-types/purchase-loan/
- KHC offers first mortgages for first-time and repeat buyers, Regular DAP (must be combined with a KHC first mortgage), a participating lender list, and a list of homebuyer education agencies. Source: https://www.fdic.gov/resources/bankers/affordable-mortgage-lending-center/guide/part-2-docs/kentucky-housing-corporation.pdf
- Peak PCS season is "the three-month window during summer months when many military families move." Source: https://www.army.mil/article-amp/256301/its_your_move_army_transportation_process_explained
