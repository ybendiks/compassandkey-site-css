Living in Irvington, KY

Irvington is a small home rule-class city in northeastern Breckinridge County, near the Meade County line and about nine miles northeast of Hardinsburg, the county seat. It began as a railroad town in the late 1880s, named for Irvington, New York, and its old brick commercial blocks along the tracks now make up the Irvington Historic District on the National Register of Historic Places. Buyers come here for small-town living with a straight shot toward Brandenburg and Fort Knox.

Getting around

  • Brandenburg: KY-79 runs north from Irvington into Meade County and on to Brandenburg
  • Fort Knox, Louisville and Elizabethtown: US-60 heads east to Fort Knox, where it meets US-31W; 31W continues north to Louisville and south through Radcliff to Elizabethtown
  • Hardinsburg and Rough River: US-60 west (shared with KY-79) to Harned, where KY-79 turns south toward Rough River
  • Time zone: Breckinridge County is on Central time, while Meade and Hardin counties (Brandenburg, Fort Knox and Elizabethtown) are on Eastern

Schools

Breckinridge County Schools serves Irvington. Irvington Elementary School is in town, and older students attend Breckinridge County Middle School and Breckinridge County High School, both on US-60 in Harned. When school assignment matters to you, we confirm it with the district for each home before you write an offer.

Choosing where to buy in Irvington

  • Inside or outside city limits? Homes inside Irvington pay city property tax and get city services, which include police, fire, electric and trash. Homes in the county often have more land and may rely on different utility providers.
  • Older or newer? You'll find older homes on the streets around the historic downtown and newer or rural builds out in the county.
  • Which way do you drive? Fort Knox and Elizabethtown commuters usually want quick access to US-60 east; Brandenburg commuters, to KY-79 north.

Everyday life

Irvington has its own city hall, police and fire departments, and a branch of the Breckinridge County Public Library. Holy Guardian Angel Catholic Church has been on High Street since the 1930s. For county offices and more shopping, residents head to Hardinsburg, Brandenburg or the Fort Knox and Radcliff area.
