Living in Irvington, KY

Irvington is a small home rule-class city in northeastern Breckinridge County, near the Meade County line and about nine miles northeast of Hardinsburg, the county seat. It began as a railroad town in the late 1880s, named for Irvington, New York, and its old brick commercial blocks along the tracks now make up the Irvington Historic District on the National Register of Historic Places.

Getting around

  • Brandenburg: KY-79 runs north from Irvington into Meade County and on to Brandenburg
  • Hardinsburg and Rough River: US-60 west (shared with KY-79) to Harned, where KY-79 turns south toward Rough River
  • Time zone: Breckinridge County is on Central time, while Meade and Hardin counties (Brandenburg, Fort Knox and Elizabethtown) are on Eastern

Schools

Breckinridge County Schools serves Irvington. Irvington Elementary School is in town, and older students attend Breckinridge County Middle School and Breckinridge County High School, both on US-60 in Harned. Everyday life

Irvington has its own city hall, police and fire departments, and a branch of the Breckinridge County Public Library. Holy Guardian Angel Catholic Church has been on High Street since the 1930s.
