Living in Crestwood, KY

Crestwood is a home rule-class city in southern Oldham County, just outside Louisville's Northeast End. It began as a railroad stop called Beard's Station in the 1850s, took the name Crestwood in 1909 and incorporated as a city in 1970. Today it's a Louisville suburb with its own city government, parks and library branch, and quick routes into Jefferson County. Our team is based in Elizabethtown and serves buyers and sellers across the Louisville area, including Oldham County.

Getting around

  • Downtown Louisville: roughly 20 miles, about 25 minutes via I-71 outside rush hour (longer at peak times)
  • I-71: KY 329 connects Crestwood to I-71 at Exit 14
  • KY 22 and KY 146: both run through Crestwood toward Louisville, KY 22 as Brownsboro Road and KY 146 as LaGrange Road through Pewee Valley, and both meet the Gene Snyder Freeway (I-265)
  • La Grange: northeast via I-71 or KY 146, home to Oldham County government offices and the county hospital

Schools

Crestwood is served by Oldham County Schools. South Oldham High School, home of the Dragons, is in Crestwood on Veterans Memorial Parkway. Elementary schools serving the area include Crestwood, Camden Station and Kenwood Station, with South Oldham Middle among the middle schools. Depending on the address, some homes with a Crestwood mailing address can be zoned for other Oldham County schools, including Oldham County High or North Oldham High, so we check each home's school assignment with the district before you write an offer.

Choosing where to buy in Crestwood

Buyers here usually narrow their search with a few practical questions, and we help you answer each one for every home:

  • Inside or outside city limits? A Crestwood mailing address doesn't always mean the home is inside the city. Whether a home is inside city limits can affect its tax bill and services, so we confirm it for every address. Homes outside are governed by Oldham County.
  • Which schools? Elementary, middle and high school assignments in Oldham County depend on the street address, so we confirm each one.
  • Older or newer? Crestwood grew outward from its 19th-century rail stop, so home ages vary a lot. We pull the build year for every home you consider and help you weigh what that means for upkeep.
  • Which way do you drive? If you commute downtown, being near KY 329 and I-71 Exit 14 helps. For the East End, KY 22 or KY 146 toward the Gene Snyder may be the faster route.

Work, healthcare and everyday life

Baptist Health La Grange, a full-service hospital with a 24-hour emergency department, serves Oldham County and eastern Jefferson County. Around town, The Maples Park on Highway 22, the city's first park and home to City Hall, has a splash park, the Deibel Barn and a playhouse building. Veterans Memorial Park of Kentucky is on Dragon Road, and Oldham County runs Briar Hill Park and Peggy Baker Park. Yew Dell Botanical Gardens on Old LaGrange Road is listed on the National Register of Historic Places, and the South Oldham Library serves the area.
