Elizabethtown Real Estate FAQ

Is Elizabethtown, KY a good place to live?

For many buyers, yes. Elizabethtown is the county seat of Hardin County, sits where I-65 meets the Western Kentucky and Bluegrass Parkways, and is a short drive from both Louisville and Fort Knox. It has two public school districts, a regional hospital (Baptist Health Hardin), a community college and university campus, and parks like Freeman Lake. Whether it's the right fit depends on your commute, schools and budget, and we're happy to talk through that with you.

What is Elizabethtown, KY known for?

Elizabethtown is rich in history. Thomas Lincoln, Abraham Lincoln's father, lived and worked here, and the Lincoln Birthplace and Boyhood Home are nearby in Hodgenville. A cannonball from John Hunt Morgan's 1862 Civil War raid is still mounted on a building on the Public Square, and General George Custer was stationed here in the 1870s. Today the town is known for Freeman Lake Park, Swope's Cars of Yesteryear Museum, the Brown-Pusey House, the Historic State Theater, the Kentucky High School Basketball Hall of Fame and the summer Cruisin' the Heartland car show.

How do property taxes work in Elizabethtown and Hardin County?

The Hardin County Property Valuation Administrator (PVA) assesses each property's fair cash value as of January 1. The PVA doesn't set rates or collect taxes; most bills are collected by the Hardin County Sheriff's Office. Homes inside Elizabethtown city limits also pay city property tax. Kentucky offers a homestead exemption for homeowners 65 and older and for those classified as totally disabled, which you apply for through the PVA. You can look up any property's assessment on the Hardin County PVA website.

What help is available for first-time homebuyers?

Kentucky Housing Corporation (KHC) offers home loans for first-time and repeat buyers, down payment assistance that's paired with a KHC mortgage, and homebuyer education. These programs go through participating lenders, so the first step is talking with a lender who works with KHC. We can connect you with local lenders who know these programs.

Can I buy in Elizabethtown with a VA loan and no down payment?

Often, yes. According to the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, eligible buyers don't need a down payment as long as the price doesn't exceed the appraised value, there's no private mortgage insurance, and the seller may pay closing costs. A one-time VA funding fee may apply. The home must pass a VA appraisal, which checks that it's safe, sound and sanitary. What is the hardest month to sell a house?

Nationally, sales data consistently shows late fall and winter, especially December, as the slowest time to sell, with late spring and early summer the strongest. Homes still sell year-round in Elizabethtown, and a slower season can mean less competition. Pricing and presentation matter more than the calendar, and we can help you plan the timing.
