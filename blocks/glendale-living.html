Living in Glendale, KY

Glendale is a small, unincorporated railroad town in Hardin County, about five miles south of Elizabethtown. It started out as Walker's Station, named for Lewis B. Walker's store, and later took the name of its station on the Louisville and Nashville Railroad. Today the old downtown along Main Street is the Glendale Historic District, which is listed on the National Register of Historic Places, and the antique shops and restaurants near the tracks draw visitors from across the region. Buyers who choose Glendale get a quiet, historic setting with quick access to I-65.

Getting around

  • Elizabethtown: about five miles north
  • I-65: Exit 86 connects to KY-222, which was widened to four lanes across the interchange. US-31W (South Dixie Highway) also meets this interchange
  • Fort Knox and Louisville: head north through Elizabethtown, on I-65 for Louisville or on US-31W toward Radcliff and Fort Knox

Schools

Glendale is served by Hardin County Schools. The town once had its own elementary and high school, and over the years its high school students were consolidated into Central Hardin High School. The former East Hardin Middle School campus in Glendale closed when the school moved to Elizabethtown. Hardin County assigns schools by each elementary school's street list, so we confirm the elementary, middle and high school for any Glendale home before you write an offer.

Choosing where to buy in Glendale

  • In the historic core or out in the county? The historic district along Main Street has late 19th and early 20th century homes and storefronts. Outside the core, the area is rural Hardin County.
  • Older home checks: With historic houses, we look closely at the year built, inspections and updates such as wiring, roofing and HVAC before you commit.
  • Services and taxes: Glendale isn't a city, so there's no city property tax, and county services apply. We confirm water, sewer or septic for each home.
  • Commute direction: Being near Exit 86 makes trips north on I-65 simple. US-31W is the route north toward Elizabethtown and Fort Knox.

Work, healthcare and everyday life

Glendale's small downtown is known for antique malls and gift shops within walking distance of the railroad tracks, along with the Whistle Stop, a long-running Southern-style restaurant in a former hardware store. Each October, the Glendale Crossing Festival fills town on the third Saturday with antiques and crafts. Near the I-65 interchange, the state's Glendale megasite is home to the BlueOval SK battery plants. For healthcare, Baptist Health Hardin, the regional hospital, is in Elizabethtown.
