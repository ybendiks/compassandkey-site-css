# Bardstown city page – research notes

Raw SERPs: /home/claude/city-research/bardstown/serp_*.json (6 queries, location "Bardstown,Kentucky,United States").

## Facts used + sources
- Home rule-class city, county seat of Nelson County; first settled 1780; "among the oldest cities in Kentucky"; first center of Catholicism west of the Appalachians – https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bardstown,_Kentucky
- US-31E, US-62, US-150 meet in Bardstown; Bluegrass Parkway passes just south – same Wikipedia page
- "Bourbon Capital of the World" is trademarked by Kentucky Bourbon Festival promoters for Bardstown; KBF designated Kentucky's official bourbon festival by the General Assembly – same Wikipedia page
- Distilleries: Heaven Hill, Barton 1792, Willett, Lux Row – same Wikipedia page
- Landmarks: My Old Kentucky Home State Park / Federal Hill, The Stephen Foster Story, Basilica of Saint Joseph Proto-Cathedral, Old Talbott Tavern, Bardstown Historic District (NRHP), Oscar Getz Museum of Whiskey, Civil War Museum – same Wikipedia page
- Bardstown City Schools serves most of the city plus adjacent developed areas; Nelson County Schools serves some of the city; St. Joseph School and Bethlehem High School are Catholic schools – Wikipedia (Bardstown and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nelson_County,_Kentucky)
- BCS schools: Bardstown Early Childhood, Primary, Elementary, Middle, High – https://bardstown.kyschools.us/
- Nelson County district high schools: Nelson County HS and Thomas Nelson HS – https://www.schooldigger.com/go/KY/district/04410/search.aspx?level=3 ; Thomas Nelson HS is at a Bardstown mailing address in unincorporated county – https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Nelson_High_School
- BCS school taxes collected by the Bardstown City Schools Board of Education Tax Office (308 N. Fifth St) – https://bardstown.kyschools.us/property-taxes/
- Nelson County Sheriff collects the county bill only; PVA does all valuation; tax districts show separate City of Bardstown and Bardstown Independent School bills, and district combos prove city limits and school district don't line up (District 3 = County Schools + City; District 5 = County + BIS, no city bill) – https://nelsonsheriff.com/wordpress/?page_id=170
- City of Bardstown levies a property tax (city council sets rate each year) – Google SERP snippets in serp_bardstown_ky_property_taxes.json (Kentucky Standard articles); city hall 220 N. Fifth St – https://kydlgweb.ky.gov/Cities/16_CityView.cfm?City_ID=14
- Homestead exemption: 65+ or totally disabled, owned/occupied on Jan 1, apply to local PVA – https://revenue.ky.gov/News/pages/dor-sets-2023-2024-homestead-exemption.aspx
- Hospital: CommonSpirit - Saint Joseph Hospital - Bardstown, 4305 New Shepherdsville Rd; 24-hour ER, 24-hour birth center, oncology/radiation oncology, general and orthopedic surgery; page still references "Flaget Memorial Hospital" – https://www.commonspirit.org/find-a-location/commonspirit-saint-joseph-hospital-bardstown-213 (redirect from chisaintjosephhealth.org/flaget-about-us)
- Drive times: Louisville about 45 min (I-65 to Exit 112, Clermont–Bardstown exit); Lexington about 60 min; Nashville about 120 min – https://visitbardstown.com/plan-your-visit/
- KY 245 runs between Bardstown and Clermont – Nelson County Wikipedia page
- US-31E runs between Bardstown and Louisville through Mount Washington – https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_31E
- Bluegrass Parkway: I-65 in Elizabethtown to US-60 near Versailles (continues to Lexington); Bardstown exits 21 (US-31E) and 25 (US-150) – https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bluegrass_Parkway
