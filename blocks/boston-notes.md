# Boston, KY (Nelson County): research notes

Raw SERP summary (DataForSEO, location "Kentucky,United States"): /home/claude/city-research/boston/paa.json ("boston ky nelson county": no PAA box; Google knowledge panel says "Census-designated place in Kentucky").

## Facts used + sources
- Unincorporated / census-designated place: Google knowledge panel (SERP above); Kentucky Atlas & Gazetteer refers to "the Boston census designated place" (https://kyatlas.com/ky-boston.html). Boston does NOT appear in the Kentucky Dept. for Local Government city listing (https://kydlgweb.ky.gov/Cities/16_CityHome.cfm) or the KDE Kentucky City Designation list (https://education.ky.gov/districts/Documents/Kentucky%20City%20Designation%20list%20%201%2018%2019.pdf), while New Haven and Mount Washington do. So: no city government, no city property tax.
- On Lick Creek, about 12 mi west of Bardstown; original settlement Old Boston a little east; probably moved mid-1800s to be nearer the new railroad and escape flooding; post office opened 1831: https://kyatlas.com/ky-boston.html
- Nelson County; Mount Moriah Baptist Church organized 1802; Beam Suntory's Booker Noe Distillery in Boston: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston,_Kentucky
- US-62 route Elizabethtown (I-65 Exit 94) – Younger Creek (KY 583, Bluegrass Pkwy access) – Rolling Fork – Boston (KY 61 north, Lebanon Junction Road, to I-65/Shepherdsville) – Cravens – Bardstown; KY 52 Bluegrass Pkwy connection: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_62_in_Kentucky
- Boston School, 130 Wilson Creek Rd, PK/K-8, Nelson County: https://www.schooldigger.com/go/KY/schools/0441001089/school.aspx
- Boston School feeds Thomas Nelson High School (Bardstown mailing address): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Nelson_High_School
- Nelson County Sheriff collects the county bill; PVA does valuation: reused from Bardstown research (https://nelsonsheriff.com/wordpress/?page_id=170)
- Homestead exemption 65+/totally disabled via county PVA: https://revenue.ky.gov/Property/Residential-Farm-Commercial-Property/Pages/Homestead-Exemption.aspx (reused)
- USDA eligibility by address: https://rd.usda.gov/files/RD-SFH-PropertyEligibility1.pdf
