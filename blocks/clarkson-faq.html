Clarkson Real Estate FAQ

Is Clarkson, Kentucky a good place to live?

That depends on what you're looking for. Clarkson is a small Grayson County city just east of Leitchfield on US-62 and the Western Kentucky Parkway. It has its own city hall, sewer and garbage service, police, a volunteer fire department, a city park and an elementary school, with KY-88 running south toward Nolin Lake. Owensboro Health Twin Lakes Medical Center is in Leitchfield, and Elizabethtown is east on the parkway. We're happy to talk through whether that fits your commute.

What is Clarkson, KY known for?

Honey. Clarkson hosts the Clarkson Honeyfest each September, which Kentucky named its Official State Honey Festival in 2006, and the town has been designated the Honey Bee Capital of Kentucky. The Walter T. Kelley Company, a beekeeping equipment maker, is based here. The town grew up as a railroad stop first called Grayson Springs Depot, after a nearby resort, and was renamed for the resort's owner, Manoah Clarkson.

What schools serve Clarkson, KY?

Clarkson is part of Grayson County Schools. Clarkson Elementary is in town, and older students attend Grayson County Middle School and Grayson County High School in Leitchfield. The district also operates a Technology Center. Elizabethtown Community & Technical College has a Leitchfield campus for associate degrees and continuing education. If a particular school matters to you, we'll confirm assignment with the district for each home before you make an offer.

How do property taxes work in Clarkson and Grayson County?

The Grayson County Property Valuation Administrator (PVA) assesses each property's value. County property tax bills are collected by the Grayson County Sheriff's Office, and unpaid bills are later turned over to the County Clerk. Kentucky offers a homestead exemption for homeowners 65 and older and for those classified as totally disabled, which you apply for through the Grayson County PVA.

What is the commute like from Clarkson to Elizabethtown?

Clarkson is on the Western Kentucky Parkway east of Leitchfield, at Exit 112 (KY-224). The parkway runs east to I-65 and US-31W in Elizabethtown. Keep in mind that Grayson County is on Central time and Hardin County is on Eastern, so a morning commute to Elizabethtown or Fort Knox "loses" an hour.
