Clarkson Real Estate FAQ

Is Clarkson, Kentucky a good place to live?

For buyers who want a small, rural town, it can be. Clarkson is a small Grayson County city just east of Leitchfield on US-62 and the Western Kentucky Parkway. It has its own city hall, sewer and garbage service, police, a volunteer fire department, a city park and an elementary school, with Nolin Lake to the south along KY-88. You'll drive to Leitchfield for the hospital and bigger shopping, and further east for Elizabethtown. We're happy to talk through whether that fits your commute and lifestyle.

What is Clarkson, KY known for?

Honey. Clarkson hosts the Clarkson Honeyfest each September, which Kentucky named its Official State Honey Festival in 2006, and the town has been designated the Honey Bee Capital of Kentucky. The Walter T. Kelley Company, a beekeeping equipment maker, is based here. The town grew up as a railroad stop first called Grayson Springs Depot, after a nearby resort, and was renamed for the resort's owner, Manoah Clarkson.

What schools serve Clarkson, KY?

Clarkson is part of Grayson County Schools. Clarkson Elementary is in town, and older students attend Grayson County Middle School and Grayson County High School in Leitchfield. The district also operates a Technology Center. Elizabethtown Community & Technical College has a Leitchfield campus for associate degrees and continuing education. If a particular school matters to you, we'll confirm assignment with the district for each home before you make an offer.

How do property taxes work in Clarkson and Grayson County?

The Grayson County Property Valuation Administrator (PVA) assesses each property's value. County property tax bills are collected by the Grayson County Sheriff's Office, and unpaid bills are later turned over to the County Clerk. Clarkson is an incorporated city, so homes inside city limits may also owe city tax; ask Clarkson City Hall how city bills are handled. Kentucky offers a homestead exemption for homeowners 65 and older and for those classified as totally disabled, which you apply for through the PVA.

Can I buy lake property near Clarkson?

Yes. KY-88 runs south from Clarkson toward Nolin Lake, a U.S. Army Corps of Engineers lake. Lake buying takes a few extra checks: the Corps manages the shoreline, and private docks and other uses of Corps land need a permit or license under the lake's Shoreline Management Plan, so owning a home near the water doesn't by itself include a dock. We also check road access, whether the home uses a septic system, and whether it sits inside or outside Clarkson city limits.

What is the commute like from Clarkson to Elizabethtown?

Clarkson is on the Western Kentucky Parkway east of Leitchfield, so most commuters take the parkway east to I-65 and US-31W in Elizabethtown. Exit 112 (KY-224) lets you get on eastbound and off westbound. Keep in mind that Grayson County is on Central time and Hardin County is on Eastern, so a morning commute to Elizabethtown or Fort Knox "loses" an hour. We often help buyers compare a Clarkson home with options closer to Elizabethtown so they can weigh land, price and drive time.
