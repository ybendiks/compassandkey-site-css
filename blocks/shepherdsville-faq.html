Shepherdsville Real Estate FAQ

Is Shepherdsville, KY a good place to live?

For many buyers, yes. Shepherdsville is the county seat of Bullitt County and sits on I-65 about 20 miles south of downtown Louisville and about 26 miles north of Elizabethtown, so you get a smaller-town setting with an easy drive to Louisville jobs. It's served by Bullitt County Public Schools, has its own inpatient hospital in UofL Health South Hospital, and Bernheim Forest is a short drive south. Whether it's the right fit depends on your commute, school zone and budget, and we're happy to talk that through with you.

What is Shepherdsville, KY known for?

History and location. The area was first known as Bullitt's Lick, after salt licks found in 1773, and it was home to Kentucky's first commercial salt works. Adam Shepherd founded the town around his mill on the Salt River, and it was chartered in 1793. In the 1800s, Paroquet Springs drew visitors to its mineral-water spa, a name the Paroquet Springs Conference Centre carries today. Shepherdsville is also the county seat of Bullitt County.

What is there to do in Shepherdsville, KY?

Close to home, Shepherdsville residents use Frank E. Simon Park and the city's other parks and recreation programs, and the Paroquet Springs Conference Centre hosts conferences, trade shows and social events. A short drive south in Clermont, Bernheim Forest and Arboretum offers more than 16,000 acres with trails, lakes and gardens, and the James B. Beam distillery has a visitor outpost and restaurant. Bullitt County's Wine, Whiskey & Ale Trail links distilleries, wineries and a brewery around the county, and Louisville's attractions are an easy drive north on I-65.

What schools serve Shepherdsville, KY?

Shepherdsville is part of Bullitt County Public Schools. Most of the city is zoned for Bullitt Central High School, located in Shepherdsville. Northern areas are served by North Bullitt High School, which has a Shepherdsville mailing address but sits in Hebron Estates, and the far eastern edge is served by Bullitt East High School in Mount Washington. Elementary and middle schools are assigned by zone. Since one Shepherdsville mailing address can land in different zones, we verify school assignment for each home before you write an offer.

How do property taxes work in Shepherdsville and Bullitt County?

The Bullitt County Property Valuation Administrator (PVA) assesses each property at fair cash value as of January 1; the PVA doesn't set tax rates. The Bullitt County Sheriff's Office collects state, county, school and district property taxes, and bills usually arrive in early fall with a discount for paying early. Shepherdsville does levy a city property tax, but it's combined onto the county bill, so city homeowners don't get a separate city bill. Kentucky's homestead exemption for owners 65 and older or classified as totally disabled is applied for through the Bullitt County PVA.

How far is Shepherdsville from Louisville, Elizabethtown and Fort Knox?

Shepherdsville sits right on I-65. Downtown Louisville is about 20 miles north and Elizabethtown is about 26 miles south, both straight shots on the interstate. For Fort Knox, KY 44 runs west from Shepherdsville to US 31W near West Point, and the KY 44 exit on I-65 (Exit 117) is signed toward Fort Knox. KY 44 also heads east to Mount Washington. When you're comparing homes, we look at which interchange or route you'll use every day, since that can matter as much as the address.

What help is available for first-time homebuyers in Shepherdsville?

Shepherdsville buyers can use Kentucky Housing Corporation (KHC) programs, which include affordable mortgages, down payment assistance, homebuyer education and access to housing counselors. KHC programs are offered through approved lenders, and KHC's website lets you look up approved lenders and check eligibility. Each program has its own requirements, so it pays to talk with a lender early. We can connect you with Louisville-area and Bullitt County lenders who use these programs regularly, then help you shop for homes that fit the loan.
