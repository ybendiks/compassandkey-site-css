## Facts used and sources
- Cecilia is a CDP and unincorporated community in Hardin County, 6 mi (10 km) west of Elizabethtown. KY 86 runs through it and ends just south at US 62. It was formerly called Cecilian Junction, after the junction of the Illinois Central Louisville–Paducah main line and the branch to Hodgenville. The main line is now the Paducah & Louisville Railway. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cecilia,_Kentucky
- KY 86 ends at US 62 southeast of Cecilia. Source: https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Kentucky_Route_86. The copy uses "just southeast," which reconciles the two sources.
- Cecilia Valley Elementary is "located in the community of Cecilia" and serves preschool through 5th grade. Source: https://ceciliavalley.hardin.kyschools.us/about
- Cecilia Valley and Lakewood both feed West Hardin MS → Central Hardin HS, and boundaries follow elementary street lists. Source: https://www.hardin.kyschools.us/parents/school-boundaries
- West Hardin Middle School is at 10471 Leitchfield Rd, Cecilia, KY 42724. Source: https://schooldigger.com/go/KY/schools/0249000525/school.aspx
- Central Hardin HS is at 3040 Leitchfield Road, Cecilia. It is "within the city limits" of Elizabethtown but served by the Cecilia post office. Nickname: Bruins. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Hardin_High_School
- Hardin PVA assessment and sheriff collection: https://www.qpublic.net/ky/hardin/duties.html
- Homestead exemption: https://revenue.ky.gov/Property/Pages/Property-Tax-Exemptions.aspx
- USDA guaranteed loans require a rural area and income below the moderate limit, and the program has a property/income eligibility tool. Source: https://www.rd.usda.gov/sites/default/files/Guarantee_SFH_Flyer.pdf
- KHC offers loans for first-time and repeat buyers. Regular DAP must be combined with a KHC first mortgage, and KHC has participating lenders. Source: https://www.fdic.gov/resources/bankers/affordable-mortgage-lending-center/guide/part-2-docs/kentucky-housing-corporation.pdf
- Elizabethtown levies city property tax. Taken from the existing etown-faq.html copy and not re-verified this session.
- Baptist Health Hardin is in Elizabethtown. Carried over from earlier notes.
