## Facts used + sources
- Home rule-class city in Shelby County; about 23 mi east of Louisville and 8 mi west of Shelbyville along US 60; I-64 (completed 1965) gave easy connection to Louisville; stagecoach stop between Shelbyville and Louisville by 1825; Old Stone Inn still standing; US 60 built over the Midland Trail; Lincoln Institute founded 1912 by Berea College for African American students, now Whitney M. Young Jr. Job Corps Training Center; residents attend Martha Layne Collins High School in Shelbyville; Corpus Christi Academy is the private school. — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simpsonville,_Kentucky
- City describes itself as "just minutes from Louisville" and surrounded by American Saddlebred horse farms; city runs wastewater/sewer system and police department; Farmers' Market, Fall Festival, Light Up Simpsonville. — https://cityofsimpsonvilleky.com/
- City parks: Simpsonville Park (108 Old Veechdale Rd), Wiche Park (7018 Shelbyville Rd), farmers market at Wiche Pavilion, Light Up Simpsonville at Wiche Park. — https://cityofsimpsonvilleky.com/parks-and-recreation
- City levies an ad valorem tax on real property within the city, using PVA values, billed by the City Treasurer (not the sheriff). — https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/simpsonville/latest/simpsonville_ky/0-0-0-790 ; city minutes reference city property tax bills: https://cityofsimpsonvilleky.com/uplimg/City%20Meeting%20Minutes_23_0221%20OCR.pdf (rate deliberately NOT used)
- Shelby County PVA values property at 100% fair cash value as of January 1; does not set rates or collect taxes; exemptions require application to PVA. — https://shelbycounty.ky.gov/departments/Pages/property-valuation-adminstrator.aspx ; https://qpublic.net/ky/shelby/pva.html
- Homestead exemption for taxpayers 65+ or totally disabled; application Form 62A350 via PVA. — https://qpublic.net/ky/shelby/exempt.html
- Shelby County Sheriff "collects taxes" / look up tax bill. — https://shelbycounty.ky.gov/departments/Pages/Sheriff.aspx
- Shelby County Public Schools school list (Simpsonville Elementary, West Middle, Collins HS, Shelby County HS, etc.). — https://www.shelby.kyschools.us/
- Collins HS (Shelbyville) feeder schools: Shelby County West Middle School and Marnel C. Moorman School. — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martha_Layne_Collins_High_School
- Simpsonville Elementary at 6725 Shelbyville Road; Corpus Christi Classical Academy, Catholic, 105 Main Street Simpsonville. — https://www.privateschoolreview.com/corpus-christi-classical-academy-profile (LinkedIn lists the same 105 Main St with a Shelbyville city name)
- I-64 Exit 28 at KY 1848 (Buck Creek Road) for Simpsonville. — KYTC I-64 Interchange+Connector Study exec summary, https://transportation.ky.gov/Planning/Planning%20Studies%20and%20Reports/5-80000%20-%20Executive%20Summary.pdf (seen as search snippet via Apify; also MapQuest listing "Exit 28/KY-1848")
- Outlet Shoppes of the Bluegrass, near I-64 in Simpsonville, opened July 31, 2014. — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outlet_Shoppes_of_the_Bluegrass
- UofL Health – Shelbyville Hospital, 727 Hospital Dr, Shelbyville; 24/7 emergency care. — https://uoflhealth.org/shelbyville
- Purnell's "Old Folks" sausage had its beginnings at a Simpsonville plant. — https://shelbykyhistory.org/index.php/2025/11/22/old-folks-whole-hog-country-sausage/
- KHC: loan programs, down payment assistance, homebuyer education, housing counselors, approved lenders. — https://www.kyhousing.org/page/homebuyers
