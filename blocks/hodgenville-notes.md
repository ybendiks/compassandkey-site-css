## Facts used and sources
- Home rule-class city, seat of LaRue County; central LaRue County in valley of North Fork of the Nolin River; US-31E through center of town, 24 mi NE to Bardstown, 43 mi S to Glasgow; KY-61 to Elizabethtown 12 mi NW; Lincoln born Feb 12, 1809 on Sinking Spring Farm near Hodgenville; two bronze Lincoln statues (child and adult) on town square; Lincoln Museum downtown; LaRue County Public Library in Hodgenville; county public schools all in Hodgenville. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hodgenville,_Kentucky
- LaRue County in Elizabethtown-Fort Knox MSA; whole county in LaRue County School District. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LaRue_County,_Kentucky
- School list/addresses: LaRue County High (925 S. Lincoln Blvd), LaRue County Middle (911 S. Lincoln Blvd), Hodgenville Elementary (33 Eagle Ln), Abraham Lincoln Elementary (2101 Lincoln Farm Rd), Preschool on College Street. https://larue.kyschools.us/
- Park units: Birthplace Unit (memorial building with symbolic cabin), Boyhood Home Unit with Knob Creek Tavern. https://www.nps.gov/abli/index.htm
- KY-61 (Lincoln Parkway) from I-65 Exit 91 at Elizabethtown; park is 1.3 mi south on US-31E from the KY-61 junction; Louisville International Airport 55 mi north of Birthplace Unit; Boyhood Home ~10 mi NE of Birthplace, on US-31E route. https://www.nps.gov/abli/planyourvisit/directions.htm
- Sinking Spring visible in memorial; Lincoln Museum on Lincoln Square behind the statue; Knob Creek ~10 mi NE of birthplace. https://www.kentuckyliving.com/news/all-things-lincoln-hodgenville-birthplace-boyhood-home-and-lincoln-museum
- Lincoln Days: two-day October festival, parade, Pioneer Games, Railsplitter Run. https://www.lincolndays.org/
- City services: Water Department, Police, Fire listed on city site. https://cityofhodgenvilleky.com/llms.txt
- City levies ad valorem tax, adopts county PVA assessment, Jan 1 assessment date, City Clerk-Treasurer prepares and mails bills. https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/hodgenville/latest/hodgenville_ky/0-0-0-5221
- PVA assesses; sheriff initially responsible for collecting property tax bills. https://revenue.ky.gov/Property/Pages/WhatOfficialsareInvolved.aspx
- LaRue County Sheriff page has a "tax bills" link. https://laruecountyky.gov/departments/sheriff/
- Homestead exemption: 65+ or totally disabled; apply with county PVA. https://revenue.ky.gov/Property/Residential-Farm-Commercial-Property/Pages/Homestead-Exemption.aspx
- Baptist Health Family Medicine - Hodgenville, 145 Lincoln Dr, primary care. https://www.baptisthealth.com/locations/primary-care/family-medicine-hodgenville
- Baptist Health Hardin, hospital in Elizabethtown, service area includes LaRue County. https://www.baptisthealth.com/locations/baptist-health-hardin/about
- "Established neighborhoods and available land for those wanting a few or several acres." https://laruecountychamber.org/live-in-larue-county
- KHC: first-time and repeat buyer mortgages, DPA combined with KHC first mortgage, list of homebuyer education agencies, participating lender list. https://www.fdic.gov/resources/bankers/affordable-mortgage-lending-center/guide/part-2-docs/kentucky-housing-corporation.pdf
