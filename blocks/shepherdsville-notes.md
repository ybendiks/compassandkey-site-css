## Facts used + sources
- Home rule-class city, county seat of Bullitt County, on the Salt River, just south of Louisville: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shepherdsville,_Kentucky
- Bullitt's Lick (salt licks found by Capt. Thomas Bullitt 1773), Kentucky's first commercial salt works; Adam Shepherd founded town around his mill on the Salt River; chartered 1793; county seat when county formed 1796: Wikipedia (above)
- Kentucky Turnpike (now I-65) built in the 1950s spurred suburban housing from Louisville commuters: Wikipedia (above)
- Downtown Louisville about 20 mi north via I-65; Elizabethtown about 26 mi south: Wikipedia (above)
- Paroquet Springs mineral spa opened 1836, drew visitors in the 1800s: Wikipedia (above)
- KY 44: enters Shepherdsville as W. Fourth St, meets I-65 at Exit 117, east as Mt. Washington Rd to Mount Washington; western terminus at US 31W/US 60 near West Point: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KY_44
- I-65 KY 44 exit signed "to Fort Knox": https://interstate411.us/interstate-65-louisville-to-elizabethtown-ky
- Schools: Bullitt Central HS (in Shepherdsville) serves most of the city; North Bullitt HS (Shepherdsville mailing address, in Hebron Estates) serves northern areas; Bullitt East HS (Mount Washington) serves far eastern portions; elementary/middle assigned by zone: Wikipedia city page + https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bullitt_County_Public_Schools ; district site https://www.bullittschools.org/
- City services (police, fire, parks & rec, public works, wastewater department; Louisville Water linked for water); Frank E. Simon Park: https://shepherdsvilleky.gov/
- City levies its own real property ad valorem tax (Ordinance 023-049 "establishing the ad valorem tax rate for real property"): https://shepherdsvilleky.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/September-11-2023-Regular-Meeting-Agenda_.pdf
- Sheriff collects state, county, school, district taxes; bills typically arrive first week of October; early-payment discount; Shepherdsville (district 11) city taxes "combined with county, no separate city taxes": https://bullitt.kysheriff.org/property-taxes/ and https://bullitt.kysheriff.org/tax-collection/
- PVA assessment date January 1, fair cash value, PVA not involved in setting rates, sheriff collects: https://revenue.ky.gov/PVANetwork/PVA%20Forms/62F100(1-24)%20Understanding%20Kentucky%20Property%20Tax.pdf
- Homestead exemption 65+ / totally disabled, apply with county PVA: https://revenue.ky.gov/Property/Residential-Farm-Commercial-Property/Pages/Homestead-Exemption.aspx ; Bullitt PVA processes homestead applications: sheriff tax-collection page above
- UofL Health South Hospital, 1903 W. Hebron Ln, Shepherdsville; emergency room: https://uoflhealth.org/emergency-rooms/medical-center-south-emergency-room/ ; opened as inpatient hospital March 2024 with inpatient beds, operating rooms, cardiac labs; Bullitt previously lacked an inpatient hospital: https://www.lpm.org/news/2024-03-21/uofl-health-opens-bullitt-county-hospital-while-norton-goes-public-with-200m-fundraiser
- Distribution centers off I-65 (e.g., Walmart e-commerce fulfillment center, Velocity 65 trade center, Shepherdsville): https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2018/08/30/walmart-to-open-e-commerce-fulfillment-center-in-bullitt-county
- Bernheim Forest and Arboretum, 2075 Clermont Rd, Clermont; 16,000+ acres, trails, lakes, gardens: https://bernheim.org/visit/
- Clermont listings incl. James B. Beam American Outpost and The Kitchen Table restaurant: https://travelbullitt.org/clermont
- Paroquet Springs Conference Centre hosts conferences, meetings, trade shows, social functions; Wine, Whiskey & Ale Trail: https://travelbullitt.org/?p=46
- Bullitt County as starting point of Kentucky Bourbon Trail; trail of distilleries, wineries, brewery; Bernheim ~15 min away: https://conventionsouth.com/shepherdsville-and-bullitt-county/
- KHC: affordable mortgages, down payment assistance, homebuyer education, housing counselors, approved lender lookup, eligibility: https://www.kyhousing.org/page/homebuyers (fetched via Apify rag-web-browser)
