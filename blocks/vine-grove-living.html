Living in Vine Grove, KY

Vine Grove is a small, family-focused city in northern Hardin County, right next door to Radcliff and just a few miles from Fort Knox. The town grew up along the railroad: settlers named it for the wild grapevines in the area, and in the 1860s the community moved about a mile east from Otter Creek to sit beside the rail line. Today it has a small-town Main Street, deep military ties and quick access to the rest of the county.

Getting around

  • Fort Knox: about 5 miles northeast, on the other side of Radcliff
  • Elizabethtown: about 11 miles southeast, the Hardin County seat
  • Louisville: roughly an hour north, mostly by US-31W (Dixie Highway)
  • Brandenburg: about 18 miles northwest on the Ohio River

KY 144 is the city's Main Street, and KY 313 (Joe Prather Highway) runs through the north side of town, connecting Vine Grove through Radcliff and Fort Knox to I-65.

Schools

Vine Grove is served by Hardin County Schools. Vine Grove Elementary School feeds James T. Alton Middle School, which is in Vine Grove, and then North Hardin High School. Nearby elementary schools can feed different middle and high schools depending on the street, because the district assigns schools by elementary attendance boundaries. North Hardin Christian School is a private option the city lists for local families.

Choosing where to buy in Vine Grove

Buyers here usually narrow things down with a few practical questions, and we help you answer each one for every home you look at:

  • Inside or outside city limits? Homes inside the city pay city property tax and get city services like police, fire and wastewater. Homes just outside often come with more land and county services.
  • Hardin or Meade County? Vine Grove borders Meade County to the northwest, so for homes in that direction we confirm the county, since it decides the school district and tax bill.
  • Older or newer? You'll find older homes near Main Street and the railroad, and newer family neighborhoods as the city grows.
  • Which way do you drive? If you work on post, being close to KY 313 or the Radcliff side of town can shorten the trip to the Fort Knox gates.

Work, healthcare and everyday life

Fort Knox is the big neighbor, and many Vine Grove families are active duty, civilian employees or retirees. Ireland Army Health Clinic on Fort Knox serves military beneficiaries, and Baptist Health Hardin, the regional hospital, is in Elizabethtown. In town, Optimist Park has an amphitheater, ball fields, Rachel's Fun for Everyone Playground and a walking trail along Otter Creek, with a dog park next door. Scheible Park and Canaday Field are the city's oldest parks, and Lincoln Trail Country Club has a par 72 golf course. Each fall, Optimist Park hosts the Autumn Daze and Bluegrass festivals.
