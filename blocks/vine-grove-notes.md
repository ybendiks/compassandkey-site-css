## Facts used + sources
- Home rule-class city in Hardin County; northern part of county; borders Radcliff (east) and Meade County (northwest); KY 144 = Main Street; KY 313 (Joe Prather Hwy) runs through north side; Brushy Fork/Otter Creek; Fort Knox ~5 mi NE beyond Radcliff; Elizabethtown ~11 mi SE; Brandenburg ~18 mi NW; mayor + six-member council — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vine_Grove,_Kentucky
- KY 313 runs from I-65 (exit 102) through Fort Knox, Radcliff and Vine Grove to Brandenburg; I-65-to-Flaherty Rd stretch is Joe Prather Highway — https://en.wikipedia-on-ipfs.org/wiki/Kentucky_Route_313 (Wikipedia mirror)
- Named for wild grapevines; established on Otter Creek; moved ~1 mile east in the 1860s to be beside the railroad — https://vinegrove.org/?p=40 ; 1860s move corroborated by https://kyatlas.com/ky-vine-grove.html
- Northern Hardin County, "along the railroad", family/youth focus, Louisville roughly an hour away, deep military ties with Fort Knox, schools listed = Hardin County Schools + North Hardin Christian School — https://vinegrove.org/our-community/
- City Hall 300 West Main Street; events First Friday, Bluegrass Festival, Light Up Vine Grove — https://vinegrove.org/
- Departments: police, fire (two stations), public works, wastewater, planning & zoning; Administrative dept handles "Taxes" and "Licenses" — https://vinegrove.org/city-departments/ and /city-departments/administrative/
- Optimist Park (40 acres, amphitheater, Rachel's Fun for Everyone Playground, soccer + baseball/softball, Otter Creek walking trail, Autumn Daze 3rd weekend Sept, Bluegrass Festival 4th weekend Sept); dog park adjacent; Scheible Park & Canaday Field = two oldest parks; Lincoln Trail Country Club par 72 golf course with bar & grill — https://vinegrove.org/parks-facilities/
- Louisville drive ~37 mi / ~57 min mostly via US-31W — https://www.ustripplanner.com/trip/vine-grove-ky-to-louisville-ky (used only as "roughly an hour", matching city site)
- Hardin County Schools school list; Vine Grove Elementary feeds James T. Alton Middle & North Hardin High; boundaries assigned by elementary street lists; other nearby elementaries split by street — https://www.hardin.kyschools.us/schools , https://www.hardin.kyschools.us/parents/school-boundaries
- James T. Alton Middle School, 100 Country Club Rd, Vine Grove — https://www.schooldigger.com/go/KY/schools/0249000518/school.aspx
- Vine Grove city council approved a reduced property tax rate (Sept 2026) and residents see it on tax bills => city levies property tax — https://hardinlocal.com/daily-brief-2026-09-23/
- Hardin County Sheriff's Office collects county property taxes — https://www.thenewsenterprise.com/news/local/hardin-county-residents-take-advantage-of-mobile-tax-collection/article_f6fea51c-aba7-5438-98c0-9007dbcac013.html
- Hardin County PVA site/office, exemptions link for 65+/disabled — https://qpublic.net/ky/hardin/
- KY homestead exemption: 65+ or totally disabled, apply with county PVA — https://revenue.ky.gov/Property/Residential-Farm-Commercial-Property/Pages/Homestead-Exemption.aspx
- VA purchase loan: no down payment if price <= appraised value, no PMI, seller may pay closing costs, funding fee, appraisal — https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-types/purchase-loan/
- KHC loans, down payment assistance, homebuyer education via approved lenders — https://www.kyhousing.org/Homeownership/Pages/default.aspx
- USDA guaranteed loan: eligible rural area + income limit; address lookup on eligibility site — https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/single-family-housing-programs/single-family-housing-guaranteed-loan-program
- Baptist Health Hardin in Elizabethtown — https://www.baptisthealth.com/about/news-center/2024/baptist-health-hardin-salutes-local-military-community-this-independence-day
- Ireland Army Health Clinic at Fort Knox serves soldiers, families, retirees (TRICARE) — https://ireland.tricare.mil/About-Us
