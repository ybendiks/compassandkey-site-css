Living in Rineyville, KY

Rineyville is an unincorporated community in Hardin County, about 7 miles northwest of Elizabethtown, with Vine Grove to the north, Radcliff to the northeast and Cecilia to the south. There's no city government here. The community is represented on the Hardin County Fiscal Court, and it's named for Zachariah Riney, who was Abraham Lincoln's first schoolteacher. Buyers usually choose Rineyville for a rural, country setting that's still a short drive from Elizabethtown, Radcliff and Fort Knox.

Getting around

  • Elizabethtown: about 7 miles southeast, mainly on KY-1600 (Rineyville Road)
  • Radcliff and Fort Knox: KY-220 runs east from Rineyville to US-31W (Dixie Highway) on the southeast side of Radcliff. Fort Knox's Chaffee gate is on US-31W at the Bullion Boulevard exit
  • In town: KY-220 and KY-1600 share about a mile of road through Rineyville and split at a roundabout

Schools

Rineyville is served by Hardin County Schools. Rineyville Elementary, on Rineyville School Road, serves preschool through 5th grade. After elementary school, the district splits Rineyville students by home location: some go to James T. Alton Middle School and North Hardin High School, and others go to Bluegrass Middle School and John Hardin High School. The district sets boundaries using each elementary school's street list, so we check a home's address against those lists before you write an offer.

Choosing where to buy in Rineyville

  • Which high school? Because Rineyville is split between North Hardin and John Hardin feeder schools, this is the first thing to check if schools matter to you.
  • County services: Rineyville isn't inside any city, so there's no city property tax. Kentucky State Police Post 4 handles law enforcement, and fire protection comes from the volunteer Rineyville Fire Department. For each home, we confirm utilities and whether it's on sewer or septic.
  • Which way do you drive? Homes near KY-220 make the trip toward Radcliff and Fort Knox easier. Homes closer to KY-1600 suit an Elizabethtown commute.

Work, healthcare and everyday life

Rineyville is a small community, so everyday shopping and services are mostly in Elizabethtown and Radcliff. Fort Knox is the region's largest employer. Baptist Health Hardin in Elizabethtown is the regional hospital, and soldiers, families and retirees with TRICARE can use Ireland Army Health Clinic on Fort Knox. The Paducah and Louisville Railway runs north to south through the community.
