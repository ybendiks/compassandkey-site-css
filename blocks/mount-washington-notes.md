## Facts used + sources
- Home rule-class city, Bullitt County, Mayor-Council government; northeastern Bullitt County; Louisville directly north; downtown Louisville 20 mi NW; Bardstown 19 mi S: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Washington,_Kentucky
- Origin as "The Crossroads" (Bardstown–Louisville road x Shepherdsville–Shelbyville road, now US-31E and KY 44); renamed from Mt. Vernon because another city had the name when applying for a post office; incorporated 1833; Confederate cavalry briefly occupied town in 1862: https://mtwashingtonky.org/residents/new_page_(2).php and Wikipedia (above)
- Growth as a commuter town began late 1960s with the opening of GE's Appliance Park in Louisville: Wikipedia (above). (Population figures deliberately NOT used.)
- US-31EX and KY 44 intersect in the heart of the city: Wikipedia (above)
- US-31E route through Mount Washington to Louisville; crosses I-265 (Gene Snyder Freeway, I-265 exit 7) in Jefferson County; Bardstown Road in Louisville: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_31E
- KY 44 west to Shepherdsville / I-65 Exit 117, continuing to US-31W near West Point, signed toward Fort Knox: reused from verified Shepherdsville research (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KY_44 , https://interstate411.us/interstate-65-louisville-to-elizabethtown-ky)
- Bullitt County Public Schools serve the city; four elementary (Old Mill, Mount Washington, Pleasant Grove, Crossroads), two middle (Mount Washington Middle, Eastside Middle), one high school in city limits: Wikipedia (above)
- Bullitt East High School, 11450 Highway 44 East, Mount Washington, BCPS, grades 9-12: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bullitt_East_High_School ; Mt. Washington Elementary (9234 KY-44 E) and Eastside Middle (6925 Hwy 44 E) on bullittschools.org per SERP
- City property tax: city bills separately from the county; residents in city limits get a city bill and a Bullitt County Sheriff bill; mailed by Oct 1; discount through Nov 30; face value by Dec 31; penalties from Jan 1; pay by mail, at City Hall, drop boxes, phone: https://mtwashingtonky.org/residents/property_tax.php
- City services (garbage & recycling, public works, stormwater, parks & rec, police department drop box): https://mtwashingtonky.org/residents/new_page_(2).php nav + property tax page
- Parks and Rec: city pool, Frank Cornell Flying Field: https://mtwashingtonky.org/departments/parks_and_recreation/index.php
- Three NRHP homes; Lloyd House cared for by Mount Washington Historical Society; Bullitt County Public Library branch: Wikipedia (above)
- UofL Health South Hospital in Shepherdsville: reused from Shepherdsville research (https://uoflhealth.org/emergency-rooms/medical-center-south-emergency-room/)
- Bullitt County Sheriff collects state/county/school/district taxes; PVA assesses as of Jan 1, doesn't set rates; homestead exemption 65+/totally disabled via PVA: reused from Shepherdsville research (https://bullitt.kysheriff.org/property-taxes/ , https://revenue.ky.gov/Property/Residential-Farm-Commercial-Property/Pages/Homestead-Exemption.aspx)
- KHC programs via approved lenders: reused from Shepherdsville research (https://www.kyhousing.org/page/homebuyers)
- USDA property eligibility is checked by full address on USDA's eligibility tool; final determination by USDA: https://rd.usda.gov/files/RD-SFH-PropertyEligibility1.pdf
