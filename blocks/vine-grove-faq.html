Vine Grove Real Estate FAQ

Is Vine Grove, KY a good place to live?

For many buyers, yes. Vine Grove is a small, family-focused city in northern Hardin County, about 5 miles from Fort Knox and 11 miles from Elizabethtown. It's served by Hardin County Schools, has its own police and fire departments, and has parks like Optimist Park with ball fields, a playground, a dog park and a trail along Otter Creek. Many residents have military ties. Whether it's the right fit depends on your commute, schools and the kind of home you want, and we're happy to talk that through with you.

Is Vine Grove a city, and what county is it in?

Yes. Vine Grove is a home rule-class city in Hardin County, Kentucky, with a mayor and six-member city council and a City Hall at 300 West Main Street. It sits in the northern part of the county, bordering Radcliff to the east and Meade County to the northwest. Because the county line is close, we confirm whether a home is in Hardin or Meade County, and inside or outside Vine Grove city limits, since that affects schools, services and taxes.

How far is Vine Grove from Louisville and Fort Knox?

Fort Knox is about 5 miles northeast of Vine Grove, on the other side of Radcliff, and KY 313 (Joe Prather Highway) runs from the north side of town through Radcliff and Fort Knox to I-65. Louisville is roughly an hour's drive north, most of it on US-31W (Dixie Highway). Elizabethtown, the county seat, is about 11 miles southeast. If you commute, we'll help you weigh which side of town puts you closest to your route.

What schools serve Vine Grove, KY?

Vine Grove is in Hardin County Schools. Vine Grove Elementary School feeds James T. Alton Middle School, located in Vine Grove, and North Hardin High School. The district assigns schools by elementary attendance boundaries published as street lists, and some nearby elementary schools feed different middle or high schools depending on the street. North Hardin Christian School is a private option the city lists. We check every home's street against the district's boundary lists before you make an offer.

How do property taxes work in Vine Grove?

The Hardin County Property Valuation Administrator (PVA) assesses property values, and county property tax bills are collected by the Hardin County Sheriff's Office. Homes inside Vine Grove city limits also pay a city property tax, with the rate set each year by the city council; City Hall can explain how the city bill is handled. Kentucky's homestead exemption for homeowners 65 and older or classified as totally disabled is applied for through the county PVA. You can look up any assessment on the Hardin County PVA website.

Do Vine Grove sellers accept VA loans?

Yes. With Fort Knox about 5 miles away, VA buyers are a regular part of the Vine Grove market. According to the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, eligible buyers may not need a down payment as long as the price doesn't exceed the appraised value, there's no private mortgage insurance, and the seller may pay some closing costs. A one-time VA funding fee may apply, and the home must appraise. Our team is led by a Marine Corps veteran, and we plan offers around PCS timelines.

What is there to do in Vine Grove, KY?

Most local life centers on the parks and festivals. Optimist Park covers 40 acres with an amphitheater, soccer and baseball fields, Rachel's Fun for Everyone Playground and a walking trail along Otter Creek, plus a fenced dog park next door. Scheible Park and Canaday Field are the town's oldest parks. Lincoln Trail Country Club has a par 72 golf course with a bar and grill. The city also hosts First Friday, the Autumn Daze and Bluegrass festivals in September, and Light Up Vine Grove for the holidays.

What help is available for first-time homebuyers in Vine Grove?

Vine Grove buyers can use Kentucky Housing Corporation (KHC) programs: home loans, down payment assistance and homebuyer education, offered through KHC-approved lenders. For homes on the edges of town or outside city limits, it's also worth checking the address on the USDA eligibility site, since USDA guaranteed loan eligibility depends on the property's location and household income. We can connect you with local lenders who use these programs every day.
