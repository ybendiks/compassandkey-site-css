Living in Clarkson, KY

Clarkson is a small home rule-class city in Grayson County, about four miles east of Leitchfield along US-62 and the Western Kentucky Parkway. It has its own city hall, sewer and garbage service, police and volunteer fire department, and it's known as the home of the Clarkson Honeyfest, Kentucky's official state honey festival.

Getting around

  • Elizabethtown: east on the Western Kentucky Parkway to I-65 and US-31W in Elizabethtown. Clarkson's parkway exit is Exit 112 (KY-224).
  • Leitchfield: a few miles west on US-62, or at parkway Exit 107
  • Nolin Lake: KY-88 starts in Clarkson and runs south through Grayson Springs, Peonia and Wax, crossing Nolin Lake on its way into Hart County
  • Time zone: Grayson County is on Central time, while Hardin County (Elizabethtown and Fort Knox) is on Eastern, so plan commutes and closings with the hour in mind

Schools

Grayson County Schools serves Clarkson. Clarkson Elementary is in town, and older students go on to Grayson County Middle School and Grayson County High School in Leitchfield. The district also runs a Technology Center. When school assignment matters to you, we confirm it with the district for each home before you write an offer.

Choosing where to buy in Clarkson

  • Inside or outside city limits? Homes inside Clarkson can use city services such as sewer and garbage. We confirm whether each property is inside city limits and how it's served.
  • Which school? We confirm school assignment with Grayson County Schools for each address.

Work and everyday life

The Walter T. Kelley Company, a beekeeping equipment maker, is based in Clarkson, and the town has been designated the Honey Bee Capital of Kentucky. Clarkson also has a veterans clinic, a city park and a community center residents can rent. Owensboro Health Twin Lakes Medical Center is in Leitchfield.
