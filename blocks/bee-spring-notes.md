## Facts used, with sources
- Unincorporated community in northern Edmonson County; county seat Brownsville; about 7 miles north of Brownsville; post office since 1854; KY 259 is the main road; KY 728 begins just south of Bee Spring and links to Nolin Lake State Park; Nolin Dam along KY 728 just southeast of town; part of the Bowling Green MSA; Central time zone: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bee_Spring,_Kentucky
- KY 259 runs from Brownsville through Sweeden (KY 728 junction) and Bee Spring, skirts the Nolin Lake area past Broadway, enters Grayson County and meets the WK Parkway at Exit 107 in Leitchfield: https://wiki.aaroads.com/wiki/KY_259
- WK Parkway Exit 107 (KY 259 Leitchfield) to Exit 137 (I-65 / US 31W Elizabethtown): https://millenniumhwy.net/exitguide/WKparkway.html
- Nolin River Lake project office: 2150 Nolin Dam Road, Bee Spring, KY; most of the Nolin River's path is inside Mammoth Cave National Park: https://corpslakes.erdc.dren.mil/visitors/projects.cfm?Id=H212760
- Nolin Lake State Park is in Edmonson County, "along the northern edge of Mammoth Cave National Park"; has camping and a boat ramp: https://kyp-kyparks-static.azurewebsites.net/mammoth-cave/parks/recreation/nolin-lake-state-park.html (static mirror of parks.ky.gov)
- USACE Louisville District: private shoreline uses (boat docks) need permits or licenses under the SMP for Barren River, Nolin River and Rough River lakes: https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20161019172340/http://www.lrl.usace.army.mil/Missions/CivilWorks/Recreation/Shoreline.aspx
- Edmonson County Schools: Kyrock Elementary (northern part of the county), South Edmonson Elementary (south), and Edmonson County Middle and High in Brownsville: https://echs.edmonson.kyschools.us/about-echs/about-us ; school list including the Edmonson Co. 5/6 Center: https://brownsville.ky.gov/our-city/Pages/Schools.aspx
- Kyrock Elementary, 5720 Hwy 259 N, Sweeden, grades PK–4: https://schooldigger.com/go/KY/schools/0162000322/school.aspx
- Edmonson County PVA assesses all property in the county: https://edmonsoncounty.ky.gov/elected/Pages/pva.aspx
- Edmonson County Sheriff's Office collects tax payments: https://www.edmonsonvoice.com/-news/2017-property-tax-bill-informations-online-lookup-and-payment ; Edmonson County Clerk handles delinquent taxes: https://edmonsoncountyclerk.ky.gov/d-tax/ (seen in search results, not opened)
- PVA, sheriff and clerk roles statewide: https://revenue.ky.gov/Property/Pages/WhatOfficialsareInvolved.aspx
- Homestead exemption: https://revenue.ky.gov/Property/Residential-Farm-Commercial-Property/Pages/Homestead-Exemption.aspx
- Hardin County = Eastern time: https://kaweah.freedombox.rocks/kiwix/wikipedia_en_all_maxi_2023-10/Time_in_Kentucky

## Flags
- FLAG: Bee Spring is **unincorporated** (Wikipedia, plus the Google knowledge graph). The page says there is no city tax.
- FLAG: Kyrock Elementary is in Sweeden, just south of Bee Spring on KY 259 (KY 259 log: Sweeden at mi 22.65, Bee Spring at mi 24.08). That it serves Bee Spring addresses is inferred from "northern part of the county" and wasn't checked against an attendance map.
- FLAG: There are two sources for the district's middle grades: brownsville.ky.gov lists a 5/6 Center, while the ECHS page lists four schools without it. Both are named in the copy. Confirm the 5/6 Center is still operating.
- FLAG: The Corps shoreline source is a 2016 archived page, so specific dock rules aren't stated in the copy.
- FLAG: The Edmonson County Clerk delinquent-tax page was not opened; the clerk step relies on the statewide revenue.ky.gov page.
- FLAG: No mileage or drive time to Leitchfield, Elizabethtown or Bowling Green came from a source, so only routes are given. The KY 259 log implies roughly 16 road miles from Bee Spring to the parkway, but that's not used.
- FLAG: There's no "Choosing where to buy" city-limits angle because the community is unincorporated. That section uses lake/park/road, Corps rules and rural systems instead.
- Left out: ZCTA population (banned), Zillow sold prices (banned), Bee Spring Park amenities (only a 2023 film-commission page; not opened), lodging/B&B listings, the Benton KY "Bee Spring" campground (wrong place).
