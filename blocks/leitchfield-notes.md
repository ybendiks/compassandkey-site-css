## Facts used, with sources
- County seat of Grayson County; home rule-class city; named for Maj. David Leitch, aide to Washington; incorporated 1866; motto "City of Twin Lakes"; Rough River Lake to the north, Nolin River Lake to the south; on the WK Parkway at KY 259; Court Square Historic District and courthouse; Twin Lakes National Fiddler Championship on Court Square; Grayson County Fair the week before Labor Day; Ice Cream Festival each July; Grayson County Public Schools operates GCHS, GCMS, H.W. Wilkey Elem., Oran P. Lawler Elem.; Leitchfield Christian Academy (private): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leitchfield,_Kentucky
- District school list (Caneyville Elem., Clarkson Elem., Lawler, H.W. Wilkey, GCMS, GCHS, Technology Center): https://www.graysoncountyschools.com/
- City at Exit 107 off the WK Parkway; "best known for being home to the Twin Lakes"; City Hall 515 S. Main St: https://leitchfield.ky.gov/
- WK Parkway exits: Leitchfield Exit 107 (KY 259), I-65 at Elizabethtown (Exit 137), US 31W Bypass to Fort Knox (Exit 136), I-165 (Bowling Green/Owensboro) at Beaver Dam: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Kentucky_Parkway
- "From Elizabethtown: take WK Parkway West approximately 29 miles"; Rough River to Leitchfield via KY-920/Salt River Road about 15 miles: https://elizabethtown.kctcs.edu/about/campuses/maps-and-directions.aspx
- ECTC Leitchfield Campus, 101 E. Carroll Gibson Blvd; AA/AS degrees, AAS Business Administration, continuing education: https://elizabethtown.kctcs.edu/about/campuses/leitchfield.aspx
- Owensboro Health Twin Lakes Medical Center, Leitchfield hospital serving Grayson and surrounding counties (formerly Twin Lakes Regional Medical Center): https://www.owensborohealth.org/news-events/news-media/2021/twin-lakes-regional-medical-center-joins-owensboro-health
- Rough River Lake spans Breckinridge, Hardin and Grayson counties; managed by USACE and the Commonwealth; Rough River Dam State Resort Park: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rough_River_Lake
- Nolin River Lake: Edmonson, Grayson, Hart counties; USACE impoundment; Moutardier ~10 mi SSE of Leitchfield on KY 259; Nolin Lake State Park listed nearby: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nolin_River_Lake
- Two USACE lakes in Grayson County; Clarkson and Caneyville are other county towns: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grayson_County,_Kentucky
- Rough River Dam SRP has a lodge and marina: https://kyp-kyparks-static.azurewebsites.net/falls-rough/parks/resort/rough-river-dam-state-resort-park.html (appears to be a static mirror of parks.ky.gov)
- Grayson County Sheriff's Office collects property taxes; unpaid bills go to the Grayson County Court Clerk after the delinquency date: https://www.k105.com/?p=95603 ; sheriff collects for local taxing entities (schools, library): https://www.k105.com/?p=7995
- Leitchfield City Council sets a city property tax rate each year: https://www.messenger-inquirer.com/grayson_county/news/leitchfield-city-council-sets-2025-tax-rates/article_d96949a8-1a85-5510-aeb3-9f66aa2cd046.html ; the city also publishes its own delinquent property tax list (leitchfield.ky.gov PDFs seen in search results)
- Homestead exemption (65+ or totally disabled; Form 62A350; file with the county PVA): https://revenue.ky.gov/Property/Residential-Farm-Commercial-Property/Pages/Homestead-Exemption.aspx
