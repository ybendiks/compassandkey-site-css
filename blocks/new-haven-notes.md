## Facts used + sources
- Home rule-class city, Nelson County; next to the Nelson–LaRue county line at US-31E / KY 52; Rolling Fork on the west side; incorporated 1839; named for New Haven, Connecticut; Abbey of Our Lady of Gethsemani (Thomas Merton); Kentucky Railway Museum with "Old 152", Kentucky's official steam locomotive; Nelson County Public Library branch: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Haven,_Kentucky
- First called Pottinger's Landing, founded 1820; Col. Samuel Pottinger envisioned a shipping point for goods bound for New Orleans; flatboat landing and warehouses near the Rolling Fork; incorporated 1839; 14 mi south of Bardstown, 12 mi north of Hodgenville; City Hall, 302 Center Street; Police Department, Volunteer Fire Departments, Recreation: https://newhaven.ky.gov/our-city/Pages/default.aspx
- City services listed: Sewer (Water-and-Sewer page), Garbage, Emergency Medical; online payments for sewer, business license, property taxes, ball forms, pool parties: https://newhaven.ky.gov/services/Pages/default.aspx
- City of New Haven property tax payable online, by mail (P.O. Box 98) or at City Hall: https://newhaven.ky.gov/services/Pages/Property-Taxes.aspx
- Kentucky Railway Museum moved to permanent facilities in New Haven, opened July 4, 1990; owns its own railroad; L&N 152 runs through the Rolling Fork River valley: https://kyrail.org/history
- The New Haven School, 489 High St, K-8, Nelson County School District: https://schooldigger.com/go/KY/schools/0441001937/school.aspx
- Thomas Nelson High School (Nelson County School District, 150 Generals Blvd, Bardstown mailing address, unincorporated county); feeder schools include New Haven School and Boston School: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Nelson_High_School
- US-31E route: Hodgenville – New Haven (KY 52 junction) – Bardstown – Mount Washington – Louisville: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Route_31E
- KY 61 Hodgenville to Elizabethtown (~12 mi NW), I-65 Exit 91: reused from Hodgenville research (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hodgenville,_Kentucky , https://www.nps.gov/abli/planyourvisit/directions.htm)
- Nelson County PVA does valuation; Sheriff collects the county bill: reused from Bardstown research (https://nelsonsheriff.com/wordpress/?page_id=170)
- Homestead exemption 65+/totally disabled via county PVA: https://revenue.ky.gov/Property/Residential-Farm-Commercial-Property/Pages/Homestead-Exemption.aspx (reused)
- KHC programs via approved lenders: https://www.kyhousing.org/page/homebuyers (reused)
- USDA eligibility by address: https://rd.usda.gov/files/RD-SFH-PropertyEligibility1.pdf
