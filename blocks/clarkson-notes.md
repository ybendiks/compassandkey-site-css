## Facts used, with sources
- Home rule-class city in Grayson County; directly east of Leitchfield along US 62 and the WK Parkway; first called Grayson Springs Depot after a nearby resort, renamed in 1882 for resort owner Manoah Clarkson; Honeyfest named "Official State Honey Festival of Kentucky" (HB 46, 2006); Walter T. Kelley Company (beekeeping equipment) based in Clarkson: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarkson,_Kentucky
- Incorporated; current class Home Rule; county Grayson: https://web.sos.ky.gov/land/cities.aspx?CTR=71
- "About four miles east of Leitchfield"; designated Honey Bee Capital of Kentucky; grew around an Elizabethtown and Paducah Railroad station: https://www.kyatlas.com/ky-clarkson.html
- City operates its own sewer system and garbage service; community center for rent; elementary school; Veterans Clinic; park; volunteer fire department; on Highway 62, Exit 112 off the WK Parkway; City Hall 213 Millerstown St: https://clarkson.ky.gov/
- Police Department, Fire & Rescue, Parks & Recreation departments: https://clarkson.ky.gov/Pages/About-Us.aspx
- Honeyfest held in September (event listing): https://visitgrayson.com/event/clarkson-state-honeyfestival/
- WK Parkway Exit 112 = KY 224 Clarkson/Millerstown, partial (westbound exit, eastbound entrance); Exit 107 = KY 259 Leitchfield; Exit 136 US 31W Bypass to Fort Knox; Exit 137 I-65 / US 31W Elizabethtown: https://millenniumhwy.net/exitguide/WKparkway.html
- KY 88 begins at US 62 in Clarkson, passes Grayson Springs, Peonia and Wax, crosses Nolin Lake into Hart County; KY 889 serves a marina and recreation area: https://wiki.aaroads.com/wiki/Kentucky_Route_88
- Grayson County = Central time; Hardin and Meade = Eastern: https://kaweah.freedombox.rocks/kiwix/wikipedia_en_all_maxi_2023-10/Time_in_Kentucky (Wikipedia "Time in Kentucky" mirror)
- Grayson County Schools list includes Clarkson Elementary, GCMS, GCHS, Technology Center: https://www.graysoncountyschools.com/ (verified for the Leitchfield page); GCHS/GCMS in Leitchfield: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leitchfield,_Kentucky
- ECTC Leitchfield campus: https://elizabethtown.kctcs.edu/about/campuses/leitchfield.aspx (from Leitchfield page)
- Owensboro Health Twin Lakes Medical Center is in Leitchfield (from Leitchfield page research)
- Nolin River Lake is a USACE lake (Edmonson, Grayson, Hart counties): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nolin_River_Lake (from Leitchfield page)
- Louisville District USACE: private shoreline uses such as boat docks are authorized by permits and licenses under the Shoreline Management Plan for Barren River, Nolin River and Rough River Lakes; some work needs a permit even on your own property: https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20161019172340/http://www.lrl.usace.army.mil/Missions/CivilWorks/Recreation/Shoreline.aspx
- PVA assesses; sheriff initially collects bills; delinquent bills go to the county clerk: https://revenue.ky.gov/Property/Pages/WhatOfficialsareInvolved.aspx ; Grayson County Sheriff collects: https://www.k105.com/?p=95603
- Homestead exemption (65+ or totally disabled; apply with the PVA): https://revenue.ky.gov/Property/Residential-Farm-Commercial-Property/Pages/Homestead-Exemption.aspx
