Living in Simpsonville, KY

Simpsonville is a small home rule city in western Shelby County, about 23 miles east of Louisville and 8 miles west of Shelbyville along US-60. It started as a stagecoach stop between those two towns, and today it sits right on I-64, which is why so many buyers who work in the Louisville area look here. The city describes itself as just minutes from Louisville and surrounded by American Saddlebred horse farms, and that mix of easy commute and open country is a big part of its appeal.

Getting around

  • Louisville: about 23 miles west, by I-64 or along US-60 (Shelbyville Road)
  • Shelbyville: about 8 miles east on US-60, home of the county offices and the hospital
  • I-64 access: Exit 28 at KY 1848 (Buck Creek Road) serves Simpsonville
  • Elizabethtown: our office is in E-town, southwest of Louisville via I-65, and our agents regularly cover the Louisville area, including Shelby County

Schools

Simpsonville is served by Shelby County Public Schools. Simpsonville Elementary is in town on Shelbyville Road, and Simpsonville students go on to Martha Layne Collins High School in Shelbyville, whose feeder schools include Shelby County West Middle School. The district assigns schools by address, so we confirm a home's zoning with the district before you write an offer. For private school, Corpus Christi Classical Academy, a Catholic classical school, is on Main Street in Simpsonville.

Choosing where to buy in Simpsonville

Buyers here usually narrow things down with a few practical questions, and we help you answer each one for every home you look at:

  • Inside or outside city limits? Homes inside the city pay a city property tax on top of county taxes and can connect to city services such as the city-run sewer system and city police. Homes outside the limits are under county services and often come with more land.
  • Town or country? Much of the land around Simpsonville is farm and horse country, so a short drive out of town can mean acreage, wells or septic systems, and rural roads. We check utilities and septic details before you commit.
  • Older or newer? The historic core grew up along US-60, while homes farther from that corridor tend to be newer. Age affects everything from inspections to insurance, and we flag what to look for.
  • Which way do you drive? If you commute into Louisville every day, being close to Exit 28 or Shelbyville Road can make a real difference.

Work, healthcare and everyday life

Many Simpsonville residents commute into the Louisville area, while locally the Outlet Shoppes of the Bluegrass, which opened near I-64 in 2014, draws shoppers from across the region. The Whitney M. Young Jr. Job Corps Center sits on the grounds of the former Lincoln Institute, a historic boarding school for African American students. The nearest hospital is UofL Health – Shelbyville Hospital, with 24/7 emergency care. Closer to home, the city runs Simpsonville Park and Wiche Park, a seasonal farmers' market, a fall festival, and the Light Up Simpsonville holiday event.
