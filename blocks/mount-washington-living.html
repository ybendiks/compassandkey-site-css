Living in Mount Washington, KY

Mount Washington is a city in northeastern Bullitt County, with Louisville directly to the north and Bardstown to the south. It started as "The Crossroads," where the old road from Bardstown to Louisville met the road from Shepherdsville to Shelbyville. Those roads are today's US-31E and KY 44. The town was incorporated in 1833 and took the name Mount Washington after the post office turned down "Mount Vernon." Its growth as a Louisville commuter town began in the late 1960s, when GE's Appliance Park opened in Louisville, and that suburban role still shapes it today.

Getting around

  • Louisville: downtown is about 20 miles northwest. US-31E runs north into Louisville, where it crosses the Gene Snyder Freeway (I-265) on its way toward Bardstown Road.
  • Bardstown: about 19 miles south on US-31E
  • Elizabethtown: west on KY 44 to Shepherdsville, then south on I-65 from Exit 117 (see our I-65 commuter guide)
  • Fort Knox: KY 44 continues west past Shepherdsville toward US-31W near West Point, the route signed toward Fort Knox

Schools

Mount Washington is served by Bullitt County Public Schools. Inside the city there are four elementary schools (Old Mill, Mount Washington, Pleasant Grove and Crossroads), two middle schools (Mount Washington Middle and Eastside Middle) and one high school, Bullitt East High School on KY 44 East. Students are assigned by attendance zone.

Choosing where to buy in Mount Washington

A few practical questions help sort homes here, and we help you answer each one:

  • Inside or outside city limits? Homes inside the city get a City of Mount Washington property tax bill in addition to the county bill, along with city services such as garbage and recycling, public works and city police. We confirm whether each property is inside city limits.
  • Which school zone? With several elementary and middle schools in town, the exact address matters.
  • Which way do you drive? North on US-31E toward the Gene Snyder and Louisville, or west on KY 44 to I-65 for Shepherdsville and Elizabethtown.

Work, healthcare and everyday life

For hospital care, UofL Health South Hospital is in nearby Shepherdsville, and Louisville's medical centers are a drive north. Around town, the city runs a parks and recreation department with a public pool and the Frank Cornell Flying Field, plus community events through the year. There's a branch of the Bullitt County Public Library, and the Mount Washington Historical Society cares for the Lloyd House, one of three local homes on the National Register of Historic Places.
