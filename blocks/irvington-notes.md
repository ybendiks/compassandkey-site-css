## Facts used, with sources
- Home rule-class city in Breckinridge County, incorporated by the state assembly in 1889; R.M. Jolly and Edgar L. Bennett bought farmland ahead of the Louisville, St. Louis and Texas Railroad and platted the town over the winter of 1888–89; named for Irvington, New York, hometown of the railroad's chief engineer; Holy Guardian Angel church moved to High Street in 1933; branch of the Breckinridge County Public Library: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irvington,_Kentucky
- "Northeastern Breckinridge county city," "about nine miles northeast of Hardinsburg," near the Meade County line: https://kyatlas.com/ky-irvington.html
- Irvington Historic District: NRHP-listed 1998, bounded roughly by the CSX tracks and Third, Caroline and Walnut streets; "most cohesive group of early commercial buildings that formed one of Breckinridge County's most prosperous railroad towns"; mostly one- and two-story brick: https://historypointer.com/nris/resource/97001342
- Schools: Irvington Elementary School, 1 Wildcat Way, Irvington; Breckinridge County Middle School, 1877 E. Hwy 60, Harned; Breckinridge County High School, 2019 Hwy 60, Harned. City Hall is at 109 W. Caroline St. Departments: Police, Fire, City Maintenance. Services: Utility Services, Electric, Trash and Recycling: https://irvington.ky.gov/our-city/schools/
- BCHS address and district: https://www.greatschools.org/kentucky/harned/160-Breckinridge-County-High-School/
- KY 79 runs concurrently with US 60 eastbound from Harned to Irvington, then north into Meade County to Brandenburg; KY 79 passes Rough River Dam State Park farther south; Harned is "southeast of Hardinsburg": https://wiki.aaroads.com/wiki/Kentucky_Route_79
- "In Fort Knox, US 60 connects with US 31W, and they remain together until downtown Louisville": https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/U.S._Route_60_in_Kentucky
- Breckinridge = Central time; Meade and Hardin = Eastern: https://kaweah.freedombox.rocks/kiwix/wikipedia_en_all_maxi_2023-10/Time_in_Kentucky
- City of Irvington levies ad valorem taxes on all taxable real and personal property (Ordinance 2025-08, first reading): https://media-irvington.ky.gov/uploads/August-4-2025-Regular-Meeting.pdf
- Breckinridge County Sheriff's Office (208 S. Main St, Hardinsburg) mails tax bills and provides tax info: https://www.breckinridgecountysheriff.net/
- PVA assesses; sheriff initially collects; delinquent bills go to the county clerk: https://revenue.ky.gov/Property/Pages/WhatOfficialsareInvolved.aspx
- Homestead exemption: https://revenue.ky.gov/Property/Residential-Farm-Commercial-Property/Pages/Homestead-Exemption.aspx
- KHC programs and approved lenders: https://www.kyhousing.org/Homeownership/Pages/default.aspx
