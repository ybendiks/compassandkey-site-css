Irvington Real Estate FAQ

Is Irvington, Kentucky a good place to live?

For buyers who want a small town with a manageable drive to Fort Knox or Brandenburg, it can be. Irvington is a small city in northeastern Breckinridge County with its own police, fire, electric and trash services, an elementary school, a public library branch and a historic downtown. US-60 heads east toward Fort Knox and KY-79 north to Brandenburg. Bigger shopping and county offices are a drive away, so the fit depends on your commute and lifestyle. What is Irvington, KY known for?

Irvington is a railroad town. Two local businessmen bought farmland ahead of a planned rail line and laid out the town over the winter of 1888–89, naming it for Irvington, New York, the hometown of the railroad's chief engineer. The state incorporated it in 1889. The brick commercial buildings near the tracks form the Irvington Historic District, listed on the National Register of Historic Places as the most cohesive group of early commercial buildings from one of Breckinridge County's most prosperous railroad towns.

What schools serve Irvington, KY?

Irvington is served by Breckinridge County Schools. Irvington Elementary School is in town, at 1 Wildcat Way. Middle and high school students attend Breckinridge County Middle School and Breckinridge County High School, both on US-60 in Harned, southeast of Hardinsburg. How do property taxes work in Irvington and Breckinridge County?

The Breckinridge County Property Valuation Administrator (PVA) assesses each property's value. County property tax bills are mailed and collected by the Breckinridge County Sheriff's Office in Hardinsburg, and unpaid bills are later turned over to the County Clerk. Irvington's city council also levies a city property tax on property inside city limits, so ask City Hall how city bills are handled. Kentucky offers a homestead exemption for homeowners 65 and older and for those classified as totally disabled, which you apply for through the PVA.

Is Irvington a good place to live if I work at Fort Knox?

Irvington works for some Fort Knox families who want a small town and a bit more space. US-60 runs east from Irvington to Fort Knox, where it meets US-31W, the main corridor through Radcliff to Elizabethtown and north to Louisville. One thing to plan for: Breckinridge County is on Central time and Fort Knox is on Eastern, so your morning drive "loses" an hour. What help is available for first-time homebuyers in Irvington?

Irvington buyers can look at Kentucky Housing Corporation (KHC) programs, which include affordable home loans, down payment assistance and homebuyer education, offered through KHC-approved lenders. Because Irvington and much of Breckinridge County are rural, it's also worth checking a home's address against the USDA eligibility map, since USDA loan eligibility is set by location.
