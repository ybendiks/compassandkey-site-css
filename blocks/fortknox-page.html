Your First Stop: the Fort Knox Housing Services Office

Fort Knox covers about 109,000 acres across Bullitt, Hardin and Meade counties, roughly 35 miles south of Louisville and 15 miles north of Elizabethtown. It's home to Army Human Resources Command, Cadet Command, Recruiting Command and V Corps, so families arrive here year-round from every kind of assignment.

Before you sign a lease or make an offer on a house, check in with the Housing Services Office (HSO). Under Army Regulation 420-1, all incoming Soldiers in-process housing through the HSO, and DoD policy says to do it before agreeing to rent or buy. The HSO then refers you on post or off post.

The HSO is free and useful even if you already know you want to buy. It can help with:

  • Rental and for-sale listings off post, including temporary lodging
  • Lease reviews, including making sure your lease has a military clause
  • BAH, TLE and PTDY counseling
  • Avoiding rental scams and unsuitable properties
  • Move-in and move-out inspections on request, landlord-tenant rights and Fair Housing complaints
  • Community maps, school-district and zoning information

Tip: Off-post listings the HSO has reviewed are marked on HOMES.mil, the DoD housing site that replaced AHRN. Current HSO hours and contact details are on the Fort Knox garrison housing page.

On-Post vs. Off-Post Housing at Fort Knox

On-post family housing at Fort Knox is privatized. Knox Hills owns, operates and maintains it, and its leasing office is on Chaffee Avenue. Off post, you can rent or buy anywhere you like, and most families end up in Radcliff, Vine Grove, Elizabethtown or the Meade County side.

Single Soldiers

Single Soldiers in grades E-1 through E-5 are required to live in the barracks, assigned through their unit. Fort Knox barracks are furnished and close to a dining facility. If you're a junior single Soldier, you don't need to house-hunt; talk to your unit first.

EFMP families

If someone in your family is enrolled in the Exceptional Family Member Program, housing requests go through your chain of command to housing with medical documentation. Start that early.

How BAH Works at Fort Knox

The Basic Allowance for Housing (BAH) gives you housing pay based on local civilian rental costs when government quarters aren't provided. Three things set your rate: your pay grade, whether you have dependents, and your duty station. The number of dependents doesn't matter, only whether you have any.

A few points that come up when families plan a move here:

  • BAH isn't designed to cover every housing cost. Budget for utilities, insurance and upkeep on top of rent or a mortgage.
  • Rate protection. Once you're here, your BAH won't drop year to year unless you PCS, are reduced in grade, or your dependency status changes.
  • Buying with BAH. Many families use their BAH toward a mortgage instead of rent. We'll show you homes that fit your number so the payment works month to month.

Look up your exact rate with the official DoD BAH calculator (search Fort Knox, KY).

Temporary Lodging While You House-Hunt

Most families need a few nights to a few weeks between leaving their old home and getting keys to the new one.

  • On post: IHG Army Hotels runs lodging at Fort Knox, open 24/7. Reservations are strongly recommended, especially in summer PCS season. DoD civilians on orders can stay too.
  • Camping and RV: Camp Carlson, run by Fort Knox MWR, has RV sites, cabins and tent camping, and some families use it as temporary lodging.
  • Off post: hotels in Radcliff and Elizabethtown are a short drive from the gates.

Temporary Lodging Expense (TLE) is the PCS allowance that helps cover lodging inside the U.S. while you move. The rules, including how many days you can claim, are set by the Joint Travel Regulations. Confirm your entitlement with your finance office or the HSO before you book.

Buying? If you're closing on a home, we line up the inspection, appraisal and closing date around your report date and TLE window, so you aren't paying for a hotel longer than needed.

Where to Live Near Fort Knox

There's no single "best" place to live near Fort Knox. It comes down to your commute, your school district and the kind of home you want. Here's how the towns around the post compare.

Radcliff

Hardin County · borders the post

The closest full-service city to Fort Knox, directly south of the post along US-31W. Its economy has long been tied to the installation. Schools are Hardin County, mostly North Hardin High.

Vine Grove

Hardin County · west of Radcliff

Elizabethtown

Hardin County seat · about 15 miles south

The region's shopping, dining and healthcare hub, with Baptist Health Hardin and a direct I-65 connection. Two school districts, Elizabethtown Independent and Hardin County, depending on the address. It's also where our office is.

Muldraugh

Meade & Hardin counties · surrounded by the post

A small city on US-31W that Fort Knox completely surrounds. The Brandenburg Station Road entrance to post is at its south end.

West Point

Hardin County · north end of post

A historic river town where the Salt River meets the Ohio, bordered by Fort Knox on the south and home to Civil War-era Fort Duffield. West Point students attend Hardin County Schools, with high schoolers at North Hardin.

Brandenburg & Meade County

Meade County seat · west of post

An Ohio River town and the seat of Meade County, which also includes the Doe Valley area. A good fit if you want a more rural setting and don't mind a longer drive. Schools are Meade County.

Louisville

Jefferson County · about 35 miles north

Kentucky's largest city, for families who want city amenities or have a spouse working there, with a commute up US-31W or I-65 and the Gene Snyder Freeway. Louisville International Airport is about 30 minutes from post.

Schools for Fort Knox Families

Where you live decides where your kids go to school, so it's worth settling early.

  • On post: the Department of Defense Education Activity (DoDEA) runs schools on Fort Knox for families living in on-post housing. Check the current schools and grade levels on dodea.edu.
  • Radcliff, Vine Grove, West Point and most of Hardin County: Hardin County Schools.
  • Elizabethtown: Elizabethtown Independent Schools or Hardin County Schools, depending on the street address.
  • Brandenburg and Doe Valley: Meade County Schools.

The Fort Knox School Liaison Officer is free for ID cardholders and is the best single contact for school questions: district boundaries, records and credit transfers, special education, homeschooling and the Interstate Compact on Educational Opportunity for Military Children.

When a school zone matters to you, we check every home against the district's boundaries before you make an offer.

Buying Near Fort Knox With a VA Loan

Buying instead of renting lets your BAH build equity, and the VA home loan is the reason many Fort Knox families can do it. According to the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs:

  • No down payment is required as long as the sales price isn't higher than the appraised value.
  • There's no private mortgage insurance, and no prepayment penalty.
  • The seller can pay closing costs.
  • A one-time VA funding fee may apply.
  • With full entitlement there's no VA loan limit, though your lender still has to approve the amount.
  • You can use the benefit again after you sell or refinance, and VA loans are assumable, which can help when you sell at your next PCS.

You'll need a Certificate of Eligibility (COE), you'll need to meet VA and lender credit and income standards, and you'll need to live in the home you buy. Ask your lender how occupancy works for your situation, especially if you might keep the home as a rental after you leave.

Your Fort Knox PCS Checklist

  1. Schedule your household goods move

    Book your shipment, track it and file claims in the Defense Personal Property System (DPS). Military OneSource covers DPS and personally procured moves (PPMs).

  2. Contact the Housing Services Office

    Before you sign anything, check in with the HSO to start on-post housing or get an off-post referral.

  3. Set your budget

    Look up your BAH, get VA pre-approval if you plan to buy, and decide on a school district.

  4. Book temporary lodging

    Reserve IHG Army Hotels on post or a hotel off post, and confirm your TLE with finance.

  5. Start your home search early

    Tell us your report date, budget and must-haves. We'll shortlist homes in the right commute and school zones while you're still at your current duty station.

  6. Attend newcomer orientation

    ACS Relocation Readiness hosts a monthly Installation Newcomer Orientation and offers PCS consultations and a Lending Closet with household basics. Spouses are encouraged to attend. See the Fort Knox relocation assistance page.

  7. Close and move in

    We coordinate inspection, VA appraisal and closing around your schedule. If your household goods arrive damaged, file a claim; the Fort Knox Legal Assistance Office can help.

Fort Knox Housing FAQ

What is the closest town to Fort Knox?

Radcliff is the closest full-service city. It borders the south side of Fort Knox along US-31W and has long been the main off-post community for military families. Muldraugh is even closer but much smaller; it's a small city completely surrounded by the post. West Point borders the north end of Fort Knox on the Ohio River, and Vine Grove is just past Radcliff to the west.

Where do most Fort Knox families live?

Many live on post in Knox Hills housing. Off post, most families choose Radcliff or Vine Grove for the short commute, Elizabethtown for shopping, healthcare and its school districts, or Brandenburg and the Meade County side for more land. Some commute from Louisville. The right choice depends on your commute, school district and the kind of home you want, and we're happy to walk you through each option.

Should I live on post or off post at Fort Knox?

On post, you lease from Knox Hills, rent typically comes out of your BAH by allotment, your commute is minutes, and kids attend DoDEA schools. Off post, you choose the town, the home and the school district, and if you buy with a VA loan your housing allowance builds equity. Either way, the Fort Knox Housing Services Office is the required first stop before you sign a lease or make an offer.

Can civilians live on Fort Knox?

DoD civilians can stay in on-post lodging run by IHG Army Hotels. Eligibility for on-post family housing is set by Knox Hills and the Army housing office, so if you're a DoD civilian employee or contractor, ask the Housing Services Office or Knox Hills about your situation. Most civilians working at Fort Knox live in Radcliff, Vine Grove, Elizabethtown or the surrounding area.

How far is Fort Knox from Louisville?

Fort Knox is about 35 miles south of Louisville. The usual route is US-31W (Dixie Highway) north, or the Gene Snyder Freeway (KY-841) connecting to I-65. Elizabethtown is about 15 miles south of the post.

What is the closest airport to Fort Knox?

Louisville Muhammad Ali International Airport (SDF) is the closest major airport, roughly a 30-minute drive from Fort Knox. From the airport, take I-65 south to the Gene Snyder Freeway west, then US-31W south to the post.

Can I buy a house near Fort Knox with no money down?

With a VA loan, often yes. The VA says eligible buyers don't need a down payment as long as the price doesn't exceed the appraised value, and the seller may pay closing costs. A one-time VA funding fee may apply, and you'll need a Certificate of Eligibility and lender approval. VA offers are common around Fort Knox, so local sellers are used to them.

What is Fort Knox known for?

Fort Knox is best known for the U.S. Bullion Depository, which sits next to the post and is closed to visitors. The post itself, named for Henry Knox, dates to 1918 and is home to Army Human Resources Command, Cadet Command, Recruiting Command and V Corps.
