New Haven Real Estate FAQ

Is New Haven, KY a good place to live?

It can be, if you want a small, rural town. New Haven is a quiet Nelson County town on US-31E, about 14 miles south of Bardstown and about 12 miles north of Hodgenville. It has The New Haven School for kindergarten through 8th grade, a library branch, a city police department and the Kentucky Railway Museum. The trade-off is that shopping, hospitals and most jobs are a drive away in Bardstown, Elizabethtown or Louisville. If that drive works for you, we're happy to show you what's available in and around town.

What is New Haven, KY known for?

Trains and river history. New Haven is home to the Kentucky Railway Museum, which moved here in 1990 and runs historic equipment on its own railroad through the Rolling Fork River valley, including the L&N 152 steam locomotive. The town began as Pottinger's Landing, founded in 1820 near a flatboat landing on the Rolling Fork, and was incorporated in 1839 and named for New Haven, Connecticut. The Abbey of Our Lady of Gethsemani, home of the monk Thomas Merton, is nearby.

What schools serve New Haven, KY?

New Haven is part of Nelson County Schools. The New Haven School on High Street serves kindergarten through 8th grade, and students go on to Thomas Nelson High School, which is outside Bardstown's city limits with a Bardstown mailing address. Because Nelson County assigns schools by address, and homes near the county line can be close to LaRue County, we confirm school assignment and county for each property before you write an offer.

How do property taxes work in New Haven, KY?

The Nelson County PVA assesses property values as of January 1; the PVA doesn't set rates. The Nelson County Sheriff's Office collects the county property tax bill. Homes inside New Haven city limits also owe a City of New Haven property tax, which can be paid online, by mail or in person at City Hall on Center Street. Owners 65 and older or classified as totally disabled can apply for Kentucky's homestead exemption through the Nelson County PVA. We confirm whether a home is inside city limits before you buy.

How far is New Haven from Bardstown and Elizabethtown?

Bardstown is about 14 miles north of New Haven on US-31E, and Hodgenville is about 12 miles south on the same road. For Elizabethtown, most drivers head south to Hodgenville and take KY 61 northwest into town, where it meets I-65. Louisville is reached by going north through Bardstown. Our office is in Elizabethtown, so we know these drives well and can help you weigh which direction you'll travel most.

What home loan options do New Haven buyers use?

Many New Haven buyers look at USDA loans, which are designed for rural areas. USDA eligibility is set by the property's address on USDA's eligibility map, so we check each home rather than assume. Buyers can also use Kentucky Housing Corporation (KHC) programs, which include affordable mortgages, down payment assistance and homebuyer education through KHC-approved lenders. Each program has its own requirements, so talk with a lender early. We can connect you with lenders who work with these programs in Nelson County.
