Living in Shepherdsville, KY

Shepherdsville is the county seat of Bullitt County and sits on the banks of the Salt River, just south of Louisville along I-65. It has deep roots: the town grew around Adam Shepherd's mill on the Salt River and was chartered in 1793, near the salt licks known as Bullitt's Lick. When the Kentucky Turnpike (today's I-65) came through in the 1950s, Louisville commuters started moving in, and that mix of small-town county seat and Louisville suburb still defines Shepherdsville today.

Getting around

  • Louisville: downtown is about 20 miles north on I-65
  • Elizabethtown: about 26 miles south on I-65
  • Mount Washington: east on KY 44 (Mt. Washington Road), which meets I-65 at Exit 117 in Shepherdsville
  • Fort Knox: KY 44 runs west from Shepherdsville to US 31W near West Point, the route signed toward Fort Knox

Schools

Shepherdsville is served by Bullitt County Public Schools. Most of the city is zoned for Bullitt Central High School, which is located in Shepherdsville. Northern areas are served by North Bullitt High School (it has a Shepherdsville mailing address but sits in Hebron Estates), and the far eastern edge is served by Bullitt East High School in Mount Washington. Elementary and middle schools are assigned by attendance zone.

Because a Shepherdsville mailing address can fall into more than one high school zone, we check each home against the district's school assignments before you write an offer.

Choosing where to buy in Shepherdsville

Buyers here usually narrow things down with a few practical questions, and we help you answer each one for every home you tour:

  • Inside or outside city limits? Homes inside the city get city services such as police, fire and the city's wastewater (sewer) department, and city property tax is combined onto the county tax bill. A Shepherdsville mailing address doesn't always mean a home is inside city limits, so we confirm it for every property.
  • Which high school zone? The street address decides whether a home feeds Bullitt Central, North Bullitt or Bullitt East.
  • Older or newer? Older homes sit near the historic downtown by the Salt River, with suburban housing that has grown out from I-65 since the turnpike era. Near the river, we also check flood zone status before you commit.
  • Which way do you drive? North toward Louisville, south toward Elizabethtown, or west on KY 44 toward Fort Knox. Being close to an I-65 interchange makes a real difference on a daily commute.

Work, healthcare and everyday life

Shepherdsville's spot on I-65 has drawn large warehouse and distribution centers to trade parks off the interstate, and many residents also commute north into Louisville. UofL Health South Hospital on West Hebron Lane gives Bullitt County its own inpatient hospital, with an emergency room, surgery and cardiac care. The city's Parks and Recreation department runs Frank E. Simon Park, the Paroquet Springs Conference Centre hosts conferences, trade shows and social events, and Bernheim Forest and Arboretum and the James B. Beam distillery are a short drive south in Clermont.
