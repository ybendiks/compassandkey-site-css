Simpsonville Real Estate FAQ

Is Simpsonville, KY a good place to live?

For many buyers, yes. Simpsonville is a small city in western Shelby County, about 23 miles east of Louisville on US-60, with its own I-64 interchange at Exit 28. It offers a small-town pace, city parks and events, and Saddlebred horse farms all around, while Louisville-area jobs and shopping stay within reach. Schools are part of Shelby County Public Schools, and UofL Health – Shelbyville Hospital is about 8 miles east. Whether it fits depends on your commute, schools and budget, and we're happy to talk that through with you.

How close is Simpsonville, KY to Louisville?

Simpsonville is about 23 miles east of Louisville. Most commuters take I-64, using Exit 28 at KY 1848 (Buck Creek Road), and US-60 (Shelbyville Road) offers a slower surface route west into Louisville's east end. Shelbyville, the county seat, is about 8 miles east. Drive times depend on where in Louisville you work and the time of day, so we suggest test-driving your commute at rush hour from any home you're serious about.

What is Simpsonville, Kentucky known for?

Simpsonville is known for the American Saddlebred horse farms that surround it and for the Outlet Shoppes of the Bluegrass, which opened near I-64 in 2014. Its history runs deep: it was a stagecoach stop between Shelbyville and Louisville by 1825, and the Old Stone Inn still stands. The former Lincoln Institute, a boarding school for African American students founded in 1912, is now the Whitney M. Young Jr. Job Corps Center. Purnell's “Old Folks” sausage also got its start at a plant in Simpsonville.

What schools serve Simpsonville, KY?

Simpsonville is part of Shelby County Public Schools. Simpsonville Elementary is in town on Shelbyville Road, and Simpsonville students attend Martha Layne Collins High School in Shelbyville, whose feeder schools include Shelby County West Middle School. Assignments are based on the home's address, so we verify a property's school zoning with the district before you write an offer. Corpus Christi Classical Academy, a Catholic classical school on Main Street, is the private option in town.

How do property taxes work in Simpsonville and Shelby County?

The Shelby County Property Valuation Administrator (PVA) assesses each property at fair cash value as of January 1. The PVA doesn't set rates or collect taxes; the Shelby County Sheriff collects county property taxes. Homes inside Simpsonville city limits also pay a city property tax, which is billed by the City of Simpsonville separately from the county bill. Kentucky's homestead exemption is available to homeowners 65 and older and to those who are totally disabled, and you apply through the PVA. You can look up assessments on the Shelby County PVA website.

What help is available for first-time homebuyers in Simpsonville?

Simpsonville buyers can use Kentucky Housing Corporation (KHC) programs: KHC home loans, down payment assistance, homebuyer education and access to housing counselors. These are offered through KHC-approved lenders, and many lenders serving the Louisville area and Shelby County work with them. If you're looking at homes in the countryside outside town, also check the address on the USDA's eligibility map, since USDA loan eligibility depends on location. We can connect you with lenders who use these programs regularly.
