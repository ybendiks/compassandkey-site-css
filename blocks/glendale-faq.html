Glendale Real Estate FAQ

Is Glendale, KY a good place to live?

Glendale suits buyers who want a historic small town near the interstate. It's an unincorporated Hardin County community about five miles south of Elizabethtown, at I-65 Exit 86. The historic Main Street, its antique shops, the Whistle Stop restaurant and the annual Glendale Crossing Festival give the town a strong local identity. There's no city property tax, and county services apply. We can help you decide whether Glendale fits your plans.

What is Glendale, Kentucky known for?

Glendale is known for its historic railroad-town downtown and its antique shopping. The Glendale Historic District along Main Street is listed on the National Register of Historic Places, and antique malls and gift shops sit within walking distance of the tracks. The town also hosts the Glendale Crossing Festival on the third Saturday of October and is home to the Whistle Stop, a Southern-style restaurant in a former hardware store.

Is Glendale, KY a city, and what county is it in?

Glendale is an unincorporated community in Hardin County, part of the Elizabethtown metro area. It isn't a city, so there's no city government and no city property tax. County services apply instead. What schools serve Glendale, KY homes?

Glendale is served by Hardin County Schools. The town's former school campus, most recently East Hardin Middle School, closed when that school moved to Elizabethtown, and the area's high school students were earlier consolidated into Central Hardin High School. Hardin County assigns schools by each elementary school's street list, so the assignment depends on the home's address. How do property taxes work for a Glendale home?

Glendale is unincorporated, so its homes don't pay city property tax. Bills cover county, school and other local taxing districts. The Hardin County Property Valuation Administrator (PVA) assesses most property as of January 1 but doesn't set tax rates, and the Hardin County Sheriff's Office collects most bills. Owners who are 65 or older or totally disabled can apply for Kentucky's homestead exemption through the Hardin County PVA.
