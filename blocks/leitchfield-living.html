Living in Leitchfield, KY

Leitchfield is the county seat of Grayson County and a small, rural Kentucky town. It sits on the Western Kentucky Parkway at Exit 107, with Rough River Lake to the north and Nolin River Lake to the south, which is why the city calls itself the "City of Twin Lakes." Buyers come here for room to spread out, lake access and a quieter pace, while staying on a straight parkway run to Elizabethtown.

Getting around

  • Elizabethtown: about 29 miles east on the Western Kentucky Parkway
  • Louisville and Fort Knox: reached by taking the parkway east to I-65 and US-31W in Elizabethtown
  • Bowling Green and Owensboro: take the parkway west to I-165 at Beaver Dam
  • Rough River: about 15 miles north via KY-920 (Salt River Road); Nolin Lake lies south of town off KY-259

Schools

Grayson County Schools serves Leitchfield and the rest of the county. Schools in and around town include Grayson County High School, Grayson County Middle School, and H.W. Wilkey and Oran P. Lawler elementary schools, and the district also runs a Technology Center plus elementary schools in Caneyville and Clarkson. Leitchfield Christian Academy is a private option. Elizabethtown Community & Technical College has a Leitchfield campus offering associate degrees and continuing education.

When school assignment matters to you, we confirm it with the district for each home before you write an offer.

Choosing where to buy in Leitchfield

Most buyers here narrow things down with a few practical questions, and we help you answer each one for every home you look at:

  • Inside or outside city limits? Homes inside Leitchfield pay city property tax and get city services. Homes in the county often sit on larger lots or acreage and may rely on county services, a well or a septic system.
  • Town or lake? Some buyers want to be close to the Court Square and shopping. Others look toward Rough River or Nolin Lake for weekend or full-time lake living.
  • Older or newer? You'll find older homes near the historic downtown, established streets across town, and newer or rural builds out in the county.
  • Which way do you drive? If you commute toward Elizabethtown, living near the parkway and KY-259 keeps the drive simple.

Work, healthcare and everyday life

Owensboro Health Twin Lakes Medical Center, a regional hospital, is in Leitchfield and serves Grayson and nearby counties. Daily life centers on the Court Square Historic District and the historic courthouse, where the Twin Lakes National Fiddler Championship is held each year. The Grayson County Fair runs the week before Labor Day, the Ice Cream Festival comes each July, and Rough River Dam State Resort Park offers a lodge, marina and lakeside recreation just up the road.
